The network computes two soft warm-chromaticity maps and local luminance contrast. A bank of hand-designed Gaussian masks looks for face-like arrangements of cheeks, forehead, chin, eyes, and mouth at five scales and across several widths and horizontal offsets. The classifier combines the strongest response anywhere in the image with a capped contribution from the amount of spatially coherent warm color. All weights come from analytic formulas and hand-selected constants; no photos or labels were used to construct them. This heuristic can miss people without visible faces and mistake warm textured objects for faces.
