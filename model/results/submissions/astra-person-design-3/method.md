The network scans an image pyramid for a localized skin-colored oval, paired eye shadows, cheek highlights, a hair cap, and a chin. Separate filters check head-and-shoulder and standing outlines with either contrast polarity. A coupled detector looks for a face above a relatively coherent patch of clothing that differs in color from both flanks; it combines the head and shirt responses at the same location before pooling. The classifier combines the strongest local matches with weaker votes for agreement across scales, repeated matches, and scene texture. `build.py` generates every filter from Gaussian probes, analytic outlines, RGB inequalities, or explicit hand-chosen rules; it uses neither training data nor pretrained parameters.
