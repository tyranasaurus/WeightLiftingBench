The detector searches an image pyramid for a face, a head above shoulders, and a clothed figure. For faces it checks that both eye regions are darker than neighboring forehead and cheek regions, and that a bridge and mouth occur at compatible positions. Other filters test for localized skin color, upper-body contours, and changes between head, shirt, legs, and background on both sides; the clothing branch can vote without a visible face. Votes are combined at corresponding locations and scales, then pooled over the frame, with weaker votes for scene context.

`build.py` draws every filter from Gaussian masks, analytic color and orientation contrasts, or hand-specified proportions and decision rules. No weights were trained, read from another model, or extracted from photos.
