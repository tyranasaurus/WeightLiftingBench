The classifier has two branches built on shared local feature maps. Fixed derivative and center–surround convolutions detect contours, bars, spots, and fine texture; piecewise-linear RGB rules detect rough color and lighting cues. One branch summarizes these maps over broad regions to distinguish settings such as sky, water, vegetation, and roads. The other matches hand-drawn geometric object models at different scales, offsets, angles, and reflections. It also checks whether multiple parts are present together, such as paired wheels below a body or eyes above a muzzle. Soft rules combine both kinds of shape evidence with the regional summaries.

All weights are compiled from analytic filters, general object geometry, and hand-written rules. Training photos were viewed as montages only to understand the task; no photo, extracted feature, dataset statistic, or check result was used to compute or select the weights. `build.py` reads no input files.
