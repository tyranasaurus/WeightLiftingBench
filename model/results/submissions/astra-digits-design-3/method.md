The weights come from hand-written Bézier pen paths and a fixed-seed geometric model that varies pen width, slant, curvature, and proportions before centering each drawing by ink mass. The network compares the drawings and the input using oriented edges and ridges, local ink density, open bays, and directional stroke ends. A grayscale flood fill distinguishes enclosed background from open bends and identifies ink that extends above or below a loop. Each class gets a whole-digit score and a part-based score: the latter allows different overlapping top and bottom shapes, provided their width and slant regimes agree. Max and average pooling combine the evidence; no training images, external fonts, or fitted parameters enter the generator.
