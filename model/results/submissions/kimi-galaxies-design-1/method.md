The network normalizes each colour channel, forms exposure-normalized luminance, and uses rectified threshold differences to make clipped faint-, full-, and core-light maps. Analytic Gaussian and difference-of-Gaussian kernels produce smooth and local-contrast maps. Normalized radial apertures and angular Fourier projections (16 phases, with a maximum over phase) provide 88 rotation-invariant measurements of size, ellipticity, dipole asymmetry, arm structure, colour, and central concentration.

The readout compares these measurements with hand-written morphology prototypes for the ten Galaxy Zoo categories. Ratios are implemented without division by pairs of rectified linear units, for example `|A/M-t|` becomes `relu(A-tM)+relu(tM-A)`. Bar, spiral-arm, merger-asymmetry, bulge, and edge-on scores use general morphology rules and manually chosen thresholds. All filters, templates, and readout numbers are generated by `build.py`; no training images, image statistics, fitted parameters, or learned weights are used. Five permitted validation runs were made, and the submitted configuration is the best validated one (32.19% training-set accuracy).
