The network is a fixed, data-free synthetic-template classifier.  It first applies a small Gaussian blur, normalizes the image, and scores it against many hand-coded stroke models at three resolutions (28x28, 14x14, and 7x7).  Each class score is the maximum normalized dot product over formula-generated variants for that digit, and the final score is the sum of the three resolution scores.

The weights are produced only by `/app/out/build.py`.  The script contains parametric pen-stroke descriptions of the ten digits (ellipses, arcs, lines, and Bezier curves), deterministic random variation from a fixed seed for slant, width, scale, centering, and style, plus analytic Gaussian rendering and pooling.  No training image, crop, patch, class average, fitted statistic, or learned parameter is read or used to make the weights.
