The detector slides head-sized windows of different sizes and aspect ratios across each photo. It combines local skin-color contrast, bilateral eye and cheek shading, oriented edges along a head-and-shoulders outline, and the colors of plausible clothing, limbs, and surroundings. For larger heads it also compares the window with analytic face drawings, both directly and after subtracting row and column means to discount stripes. Neighboring positions and aspect ratios contribute to the final detection score; bounded scene cues account for confounders such as food on plates and long architectural edges.

I wrote the geometry, color rules, thresholds, and cue combinations by hand. `build.py` generates the face filters from ellipses, smooth shading, and Gaussian features in normalized anatomical coordinates; it reads no photos, labels, or other files. The final labeled sandbox check was 742/1,214 correct (61.12%) on a random sample of the provided training split, not the hidden evaluation set.
