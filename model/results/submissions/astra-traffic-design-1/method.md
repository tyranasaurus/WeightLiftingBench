The network compares each crop with a hand-built grammar of German signs: rings, triangles, diamonds, arrows, vehicle silhouettes, warning pictograms, and numerals drawn from lines, polygons, ellipses, and Bézier curves. A piecewise-linear log transform and opponent-color channels reduce sensitivity to illumination; separate branches compare the whole sign, its outline, and the oriented edges of its emblem. Fixed bilinear sampling matrices also map candidate sign faces to a common grid, where the network normalizes local contrast and checks character parts, pictogram parts, or the absence of a mark. Max pooling permits shifts, width changes, and several drawing styles, while the final evidence must agree on a sign scale. All weights are constructed deterministically in `build.py` from these geometric and photometric rules; no training images, fitted statistics, fonts, or external assets are used by the builder.
