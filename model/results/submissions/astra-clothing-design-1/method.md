The builder draws ten families of paper silhouettes from polygons, curves, and a fixed random seed: for example, trousers have a joined seat and two tapered legs, while a sandal has straps above a sole. A dense layer compares the image's soft foreground mask and its horizontal and vertical scanline envelopes to those drawings, and max pooling selects the closest cut in each family. A second branch uses hand-specified contrast filters and bounded ReLU rules to detect construction details such as a continuous placket, paired lapels, a handle opening, and space under shoe straps. The output combines broad shape evidence with detail scores within each family. All weights come from these drawing and rule formulas; the builder reads no images or labels and performs no fitting.
