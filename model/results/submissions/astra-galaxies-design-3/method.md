The network measures each galaxy through concentric apertures and elliptical rings. Angular moments estimate the projected axis ratio; colour changes and radial ridges reveal star-forming arms; and a comparison of the core and outer disk tests for a bulge. Separate branches look for secondary nuclei and compare the light profile with analytic models of smooth galaxies, barred disks, logarithmic spirals of either handedness, and pairs of galaxies. A hand-written piecewise-linear decision rule combines the evidence into ten class scores. All filters, templates, and rule weights are generated by `build.py` from formulas and hand-selected constants, without loading images or fitting parameters.
