The network is a fixed, multi-scale shape matcher. It normalizes each image, applies a Gaussian high-pass filter and Sobel derivatives, forms eight rectified orientation channels, and pools them at 14 x 14 and 7 x 7 resolutions. Coarse normalized intensity, high-pass, and positive-intensity maps provide additional silhouette cues.

The class weights are cosine-matching templates for 48 procedurally rendered pose variants of each ASL letter. The renderer uses only a general anatomical hand model: a palm ellipse, wrist, tapered finger-segment polylines, class-specific thumb poses, mirroring, and fixed-seed changes in scale and articulation. Each modality contributes a hand-chosen gain, and a maximum over pose variants produces the 24 class scores. No training image, label statistic, image measurement, optimization, regression, or fitted readout is used; `build.py` regenerates every weight from these formulas alone.
