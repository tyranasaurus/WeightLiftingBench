# Method

This is a data-free, hand-designed network: no optimisation, fitting, pretrained weights, training-image descriptors, or training-derived constants are used. Analytic Gaussian directional derivatives and difference-of-Gaussian filters form contrast-normalised edge and spot maps at two spatial resolutions, alongside a luminance map. Cross-orientation inhibition gives selective edge-direction responses, and local maximum pooling permits small contour deformations. A bank of geometric drawings encodes generic airplanes, cars, birds, cats, deer, dogs, frogs, horses, ships, and heavy trucks using ellipses, polygons, and capsules; each drawing supplies only its own independently normalised template, with predetermined translations, scales, reflections, small rotations, and shape variations. Dense correlations followed by maximum pooling select each class's strongest geometric match, with both luminance polarities allowed. Two convolutional template banks add position-independent local-part evidence, including faces, frog heads, bird heads, and vehicle wheels.

Each candidate local patch is independently contrast-normalised using fixed extraction wiring. An additional foreground cue compares the current input with its own border and row-wise side background, and hand-written texture-density and rectilinearity rules supply modest supplementary evidence. A separate small branch implements hand-written bounded RGB-opponent colour memberships and foreground/background rules, such as blue sky around an airplane, blue water below a vessel, green frogs, and brown quadrupeds against vegetation; analytic foreground and support regions also align colour evidence with each whole-object hypothesis. All feature gains, geometric dimensions, colour thresholds, and rule coefficients are literal a priori choices in `build.py`; no statistic across generated drawings sets any parameter. The builder reads no files, generates all arrays deterministically with NumPy, and writes the network and float32 weights to the requested directory.

## Verification

The final network has 68,462,869 float32 parameters and passes `check_network.py`. Exactly five labelled evaluations were performed through `run_network.py`: four on the first 4,000 training examples while inspecting the hand-designed approach, followed by the frozen final network on the next 10,000, disjoint examples. The final disjoint check obtained 26.91% accuracy (normalized task score approximately 0.1879 on that subset); this is not a measurement of the unseen scoring set or either comparison baseline. `check_rebuild.py /app/out` passed, with zero numerical difference between submitted and rebuilt arrays.
