# Hand-designed geometric recognition network

This 36-layer network combines contrast-normalized luminance and opponent-color silhouette correlations, unsigned Sobel contour correlations, local dark-landmark detectors, soft color predicates, and long-edge scene cues. For each class, `build.py` draws three generic object models from ellipses, polygons, and line segments, then enumerates fixed scales, translations, rotations, and reflections; max pooling selects the strongest of 486 poses per class. Examples of the hand-specified anatomy and structure are airplane wings and tailplanes, car and truck wheels, bird beaks, cat ears, dog muzzles, ungulate legs, frog eye bulges, and ship hulls and masts. Every filter and compatibility coefficient is specified directly in the program or calculated from these geometric formulas; there is no optimization or fitting, and the builder reads no data or other files.

The network contains 9,356,649 float32 weight entries. No training images were viewed or measured, and no image statistics were used to construct weights. One permitted labeled check on the first 10,000 official training examples achieved 19.18% accuracy; it revealed frequent confusions between animals and vehicles, and no weights were changed after that check. This is a limited hand-designed classifier, not a claim of competitive trained-model performance or a measurement of held-out accuracy.
