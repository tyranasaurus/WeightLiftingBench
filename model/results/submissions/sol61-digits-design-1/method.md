# Analytic stroke-model network

The weights are generated entirely from hand-written, generic digit pen paths (Bezier curves, ellipses, and a figure-eight formula), with a fixed random seed for variations in proportions, slant, bending, stroke width, and raster phase. The synthetic paths are scaled and centered according to the task's stated preprocessing convention. No training image, patch, descriptor, class average, or fitted parameter is used to construct any weight; `build.py` reads no files and performs no training or optimization.

The network compares the input with 8,192 independently generated writing styles per class. Fixed convolution, pooling, normalization, and ReLU layers produce four feature views: reduced-resolution ink, blurred ink, dilated ink, and a soft enclosure map computed from four directional ink sums. Dense weights implement normalized template correlations, including a small analytic stroke-proximity contribution. The scores from the four views are added, and max-pooling over writing styles yields ten class scores. All feature weights and mixture constants are chosen by hand.

There are 64,554,490 float32 weight entries, below the 120,000,000 limit. Five labeled checks were used to identify broad shortcomings of the hand-designed model; these were not parameter searches or readout fitting. The only training-image inspection was an unmeasured contact sheet, and no individual image measurements were transferred into the generator. The final permitted check reported 87.64% accuracy on all 60,000 training images; hidden-set accuracy is unknown. Structural validation passed, and the isolated data-free rebuild reproduced the submitted network and weights with zero numerical difference.
