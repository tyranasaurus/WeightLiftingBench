The classifier matches oriented edges, local brightness changes, and foreground color against geometric drawings of the ten object classes. The build script draws silhouettes and parts from polygons, ellipses, and tapered segments, then varies their scale, position, angle, reflection, and shading on a deterministic grid. Each candidate also compares colors inside its silhouette to a narrow band outside it. Hand-written rules add evidence from parts such as paired wheels and eyes, cargo panels, and legs, and from scene cues such as sky, pavement, rippled water, or vegetation. I viewed a class montage for qualitative context; the drawings, filters, and rules are specified in the build script, with no fitted parameters or stored training examples.
