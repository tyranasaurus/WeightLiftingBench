This network first average-pools each 128×128 RGB image to 32×32, then uses a 1×1 convolution to make two channels: luminance and a red-minus-blue colour contrast channel. After per-channel spatial normalization, a bank of handcrafted 28×28 templates is applied by convolution. The templates were written analytically from general morphology knowledge using Gaussian blobs, differences of Gaussians, elongated ellipses, paired nuclei for mergers, one-armed disturbed shapes, logarithmic-spiral-like arms, bars, and edge-on disk/bulge models; some templates also use the colour-contrast channel to reward red bulges and bluer spiral arms.

There are 15 template families with 16 variants each, covering disturbed, merging, smooth round/oval/cigar, barred spiral, tight spiral, loose spiral, and the two edge-on classes. The network takes a max over image position for each template, then a max over variants within each family, then uses a small handwritten dense readout to turn those family responses into 10 class scores.

All weights were produced directly by formulas and manually chosen constants in build.py, without fitting to the training labels or storing image-derived templates.
