The network uses a 4x averaged view of each RGB image.  One branch normalizes each channel and applies a formula-generated bank of centred galaxy templates: round and elliptical Sersic-like blobs, thin edge-on disks with and without a bulge, two-component mergers, asymmetric tidal arcs, barred spirals, and logarithmic spiral arms at fixed pitches.  Responses are max-pooled over scale/orientation/chirality within each class and combined by a hand-written morphology rule table.  A second branch adds hand-coded colour and concentration cues (red central light, blue outer arms, and central excess).  A third branch uses fixed oriented centre-surround ridge filters on the original image to reward bars, arms, dust lanes, tidal filaments, and thin edge-on disks.  All weights are produced analytically in build.py from Gaussian, elliptical, annular, ridge, and logarithmic-spiral formulae; no training images or fitted statistics are used as weights.
