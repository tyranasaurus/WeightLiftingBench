`build.py` draws its own garment patterns from proportions: torso and sleeve seams, paired trouser legs, flared skirts, shoe uppers and soles, sandal straps, and bags with loop handles. It varies the cut, placement, and reflectance with a fixed seed. Dense weights and biases implement squared silhouette mismatch (up to a class-independent term), and pooling combines the best matches from four pattern groups per class. Other branches test hand-specified details such as a continuous sole, clear space between straps, a shirt-button row, and a pullover's neck and hem. A hierarchical readout keeps evidence about a fine style from dominating the decision about the kind of article. The builder uses only formulas and constants in its source; no weights were fitted or extracted from data.
