# Analytic galaxy-morphology network

This is a fixed, hand-designed network, not a trained model. It estimates a sky pedestal from an outer annulus, suppresses faint background light, and computes multiscale flux, color, quadrupole shape, central-concentration, rotational-asymmetry, and difference-of-Gaussian structure measurements. Analytic exponential, Gaussian, cusped, barred, logarithmic-spiral, and projected edge-on templates supply additional orientation- and scale-tolerant shape comparisons. Dense layers implement these fixed measurements; ReLUs implement hand-written fuzzy thresholds, max pooling selects template orientations/scales, and normalization layers approximate dimensionless ratios before a hand-written ten-class readout.

Every weight comes from formulas and manually chosen general morphology constants in `build.py`; it reads no input files and uses no pretrained parameters, optimization, fitting, image-derived templates, or stored examples. Training images were viewed as class montages, and a fixed random subset of 1,800 official training images was used for exactly five labeled diagnostic checks with the provided runner. The final submitted revision obtained 0.3228 accuracy on that subset; this is not an estimate from the unseen scoring set. The checks expose substantial remaining confusion among spiral subclasses and bulged edge-on galaxies, and superiority to the comparison systems has not been established.
