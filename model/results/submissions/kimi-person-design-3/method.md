This is a hand-engineered, multiscale person detector, not a fitted model. A luminance branch supplies globally normalized 24 x 24 and 12 x 12 images and four unsigned Sobel-orientation maps. Local edge and dark-feature responses are clipped to prevent isolated high-contrast outliers from dominating a global maximum. Two YCbCr/RGB rules provide strict and broad skin maps; both require non-neutral red chroma.

The face branch scans formula-generated ellipses over six sizes and three aspect/detail variants. It combines skin inside the ellipse, a penalty for a uniformly skin-coloured surround, dark eye/hair/mouth regions, and an elliptical contour. The body branches scan geometrically rendered ellipse-and-capsule figures at five full-body and five upper-body scales. Their eight fixed-seed pose variants cover standing, stepping, seated, bent-arm, spread-arm, and raised-arm silhouettes, with several horizontal aspect ratios. Body evidence requires a nontrivial oriented-contour response before adding skin in the anatomical head region.

Maxima over positions, templates, and scales provide translation and scale tolerance. The three colour-and-geometry evidence values are capped, weighted by hand, and combined by a final maximum and hand-chosen offset. `build.py` generates every filter, template, threshold, and readout weight entirely from formulas and constants in its own source. It reads no files and uses no image statistic, optimization, regression, or training procedure.
