This is a procedural shape-model classifier rather than a fitted network. It compares each input against a large, deterministic collection of generic handwritten-digit drawings assembled from ellipses and typed pen paths, with independently generated variations in slant, width, curvature, stroke thickness, rotation, and digit style. A fixed Gaussian/edge convolutional front end, local max pooling, and channel normalization provide some tolerance to ink appearance and small displacement; the final class score is the best normalized feature correlation among that class's generated styles. Every filter and prototype is recreated from formulas and general digit geometry in `build.py`, which does not read any data.
