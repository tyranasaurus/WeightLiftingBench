This is a hand-written convolutional template network for detecting people in small COCO photos.  The first layers compute fixed colour half-spaces for skin/warm/dark/bright/blue/green regions plus Sobel-like edge channels; later layers preserve these primitive channels while adding fixed Gaussian geometric templates for faces, head-and-shoulders, standing figures, sitting figures, groups, and a few weak animal/food/scene counter-templates.  Max-pooling gives approximate translation tolerance and the final dense layer compares pooled person-template evidence against a fixed no-person bias.

The weights are produced entirely by analytic formulas in build.py: colour constants, edge stencils, Gaussian masks, and hand-chosen thresholds from general knowledge of person shapes and common non-person confounders.  I viewed random training-image contact sheets to understand the task distribution and used the five permitted network checks only to calibrate the final threshold; no gradient descent, fitting, class averages, stored examples, image-derived templates, or copied pixel values were used.
