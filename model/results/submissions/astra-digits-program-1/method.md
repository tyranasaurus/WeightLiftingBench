The classifier compares each input with hand-authored models of the ten digits. `build.py` draws lines, Bézier curves, and loops, varying their proportions, joins, slant, curvature, pen angle, and pressure with a fixed seed. All saved constants are descriptors of those drawings; the build reads no data, fonts, or other assets.

For each input, `predict` centers the ink, compensates for slant, and partially normalizes height and width. It shortlists pen models using pooled ink density, edge orientations, enclosed background, and aspect ratio. It then compares the shortlisted models in more detail: overlapping patches may move slightly, with a penalty if neighboring patches move in different directions. Thinning each digit to a skeleton gives a separate check on where strokes end or branch. The class with the lowest combined matching cost wins. No model parameters were fitted to images or labels.
