The network has two branches. One compares a soft foreground map with procedural silhouettes built from polygons and strokes for garment bodies, sleeves, legs, footwear uppers and soles, and bag handles. The other uses local derivatives, pooling, and ReLU comparisons to score cues such as a persistent gap between trouser legs, collar and closure lines, and openings in sandal uppers. The final ten scores combine the best silhouette match for each class with those construction cues.

`build.py` generates all weights from formulas, a fixed seed for variations in the procedural shapes, and hand-written rules. It reads no input files. Training images were used only for visual inspection and the logged scorer checks; none supplied templates, fitted parameters, or weight values.
