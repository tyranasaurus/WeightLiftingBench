`build.py` draws the signs from geometric primitives: borders, typographic strokes, arrows, vehicles, people, and other pictograms. It renders these drawings at different scales, offsets, and blur levels; it does not load training images, fonts, or any other assets. For each input photo, `predict` uses brightness and color-opponent contrasts to locate candidate sign faces, then compares their interiors and whole faces with the drawings. It checks digits in separate windows and lets parts of a warning pictogram move slightly relative to each other. The training data was used for raw visual inspection and labeled checks, not to fit parameters or construct templates.
