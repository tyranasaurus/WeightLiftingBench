# Analytic person-presence network

This fixed 175-layer network combines multiscale face-contrast tests, compact skin-coloured head tests with torso support, and orientation-selective head/shoulder/body contour templates. Its filters are generated from pixel-integrated elliptical Gaussians, finite differences, and generic anatomical paths; ReLU layers implement hand-written colour halfspaces and conjunctions of anatomical conditions, and max pooling searches over positions, sizes, and aspect ratios. The final two scores are zero for class 0 and the strongest rule margin, minus a broad warm-colour-scene penalty, for class 1.

All 703,844 float32 weight entries are constructed by `build.py` from formulas and explicit hand-chosen constants, without reading data or other files, importing external weights, optimizing parameters, or copying image measurements. Class montages were viewed, and the five permitted labeled runner checks were used to identify failures of the hand-written rules; revisions added anatomical constraints rather than fitting a statistical readout. This is a heuristic detector with substantial limitations, especially for small, occluded, monochrome, or rear-facing people; there is no evidence that it beats a trained network.

`check_network.py` validates the shapes, finite weights, and parameter limit. `check_rebuild.py` passes with zero numerical difference between the submitted and rebuilt network weights. The fifth and final runner check obtained 52.75% accuracy on 2,000 index-spaced training images, with confusion matrix `[[1010, 20], [925, 45]]` (rows are true labels). Thus the final detector has very low person recall; held-out accuracy and performance against either comparison answer are unknown. All five permitted checks were used, and no inference was performed outside the supplied runner.
