I built a small hand-designed template network with two branches. One branch average-pools the image to 7×7 and applies coarse silhouette detectors; the other average-pools to 14×14 and applies finer detectors for details such as collars, sleeve length, handles, trouser leg gaps, and shoe/boot shafts. All detectors are written directly from geometric shape models made from boxes, trapezoids, ellipses, line segments, and mirrored left/right shoe variants.

Each detector is a fixed dense template with positive weight on expected foreground regions and negative weight on nearby background regions, followed by ReLU. The final class scores are another fixed dense layer that combines prototype activations and pairwise penalties between confusing classes. I chose all constants by hand from general knowledge about the item categories and by visually inspecting training images, without fitting or deriving weights from the dataset.
