# Method

This is a deterministic, formula-derived morphology network; no optimization, fitting, image measurements, class averages, or copied image content were used to produce its weights. The input is normalized channel-wise and averaged to 32×32, after which parallel analytic expert banks score generic galaxy properties: elliptical Sérsic-like profiles across axis ratios, scales, and position angles; smooth disk, bulge, bar, and edge-on models; logarithmic two-arm spirals at several pitch angles and handednesses; double-core merger patterns; rotational asymmetry apertures; and zero-mean Gabor texture filters. A separate fixed aperture computes outer blue-versus-red spatial colour contrast.

Max pooling over angle, scale, handedness, and phase gives approximate rotation and size invariance. Hand-written rule matrices map the resulting generic properties to the ten Galaxy Zoo classes, and the independent class scores are summed. The final diagonal gains and offsets compensate analytic template-bank scale differences and serve as morphology-prior tie breakers. Every numerical weight is regenerated in `build.py` from Gaussian, elliptical, logarithmic-spiral, trigonometric, and Gabor formulas using only NumPy and the task's stated 128×128 geometry.
