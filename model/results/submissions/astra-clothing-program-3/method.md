`build.py` uses a fixed seed to draw variations of hand-designed garments, bags, and footwear: for example, two tapering trouser legs, a torso with independent sleeves, or a footbed with arched straps. `constants.npz` contains only coarse occupancy and contour descriptors of these analytic drawings, their squared lengths, and group sizes. The builder reads no images or other input files.

For each input, `predict` extracts a soft foreground mask and compares it with the drawings. It resolves ambiguous outlines with hand-written tests for features such as a continuous inseam, a front closure, relative cuff and hem heights, a tongue or boot shaft, and background visible under sandal straps. Shape selects the broad family before fabric or construction details decide between similar classes. No parameters were fitted; training data was used for visual inspection and five labeled checks. The final check gave 71.18% accuracy on a separate 10,000-image portion of the training set, not the hidden test set.
