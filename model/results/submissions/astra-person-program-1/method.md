The detector searches several head sizes and aspect ratios using box averages of color and brightness. It checks candidates for a head outline, dark eyes above lighter cheeks, and a wider torso below the head; an oriented-edge silhouette supplies a separate route when the face has little or no visible skin. It also groups skin-colored regions to look for a face and limbs separated by clothing. Weak scene cues include subject-to-background sharpness, painted lines on a playing surface, and plate rims that might otherwise be mistaken for skin-colored faces. All geometry, color rules, and weights are hand-specified in `build.py`. The builder reads no data and emits only `solution.py`; no example images, pretrained weights, or fitted statistics are used.
