# Hand-designed recognition network

The network uses 76 layers and 12,557,517 float32 weight entries, with ten output scores in the requested class order. Its fixed feature extractors measure opponent colours, Gaussian centre-surround responses, directional derivatives, texture, and regional contrast; analytic ellipses and polygons describe idealized wings, vehicle bodies, wheels, hulls, and animal bodies and faces over predetermined positions, scales, and orientations. A separate branch measures each input's colour distance from its own corner regions to form a foreground activation, and min/max pooling combines part conjunctions and alternative object hypotheses with weak, handwritten context rules.

All persistent coefficients are generated by `build.py` from formulas, generic geometric primitives, and explicitly written semantic rules. The builder reads no files and uses no randomness, learned parameters, image-derived templates, fitted readout, or training-set statistics. Corner colours and normalization statistics are computed only as transient activations for the current input, not stored as weights. Training examples were viewed as a montage, and the permitted labelled checks guided qualitative structural revisions; no parameter search or numerical fitting was performed.

Exactly five labelled checks were run using the supplied runner. Checks 1–4 used the first 5,000 training images and obtained 21.08%, 20.60%, 20.64%, and 18.64% accuracy respectively. The final network was left unchanged after check 4, and check 5 obtained 18.89% on the remaining 45,000 training images. These are training-set checks, not held-out-test results; they do not establish superiority over either comparator.
