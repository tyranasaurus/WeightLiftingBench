The network combines three hand-designed feature branches. A colour branch applies fixed RGB/luminance opponency formulas, rectification, and 4 x 4 spatial averaging, followed by manually specified rules for sky, water, vegetation, object colour, and illumination. A texture branch applies unit-norm Gaussian derivatives in four orientations and two centre/surround filters, with spatial pooling and hand-written class rules for body lines, wings, legs, panels, and surface detail. A shape branch convolves normalized luminance with 160 zero-mean, unit-norm geometric silhouettes drawn in code from ellipses, polygons, and segments: four general poses for each class, two contrast polarities, and small flip/scale variations. Rectification and max pooling provide limited translation tolerance.

Every number is generated by formulas or general object-shape knowledge in `build.py`; no training images, labels, image statistics, fitted classifiers, or external files are used. The best of the five logged checks was 18.86% accuracy on the same 10,000-image training subset; the final files restore that configuration after the last alternative performed worse.
