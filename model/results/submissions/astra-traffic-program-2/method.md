The classifier searches for painted sign borders using log-luminance, colour contrast, and separate tests along different sides of a candidate border. It rectifies each candidate and compares its interior and stroke gradients with hand-drawn models of the numerals, vehicles, arrows, and warning symbols. A second comparison centers and scales the drawing using the visible ink, so an imperfect border estimate need not spoil the reading. Uniform fields, the STOP lettering, the no-entry stripe, and traffic-light colours have explicit tests. `build.py` renders the models and their transformed filters from geometric primitives; it reads no image, font, or template files and performs no training.
