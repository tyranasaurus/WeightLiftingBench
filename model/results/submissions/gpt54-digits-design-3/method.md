This network is a hand-built synthetic-template classifier. I typed four stroke sketches for each digit from general knowledge, rendered them analytically, then expanded them with a fixed library of deterministic jitters and slant/aspect/stroke transforms. The main score is a per-class max over many multiscale template matches (14x14 normalized, 7x7 normalized, and 7x7 raw occupancy), with smaller full-resolution, oriented-edge, and 4x4 occupancy branches added as priors. All weights come directly from typed geometry, fixed formulas, and a fixed random seed scheme; there is no fitting, averaging over the training set, or gradient descent.
