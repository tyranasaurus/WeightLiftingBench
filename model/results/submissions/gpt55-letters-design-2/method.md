The network first normalizes the image, applies a fixed bank of 16 oriented derivative-of-Gaussian edge filters, rectifies them, max-pools to 14 x 14, and normalizes each edge channel.  A dense layer then compares those edge features with formula-generated ASL sign templates.  Each template is made in `build.py` from simple hand geometry: ellipses for palms/fists/rings and rounded line segments for fingers, thumbs, gaps and absent-finger regions.

For robustness, every class has hand-set variants over small translations, scale changes, rotations, left/right reflection, and light/dark polarity; the network reshapes the template scores by class and max-pools over variants to produce one score per letter.  The weights are generated entirely by these analytic formulas and constants in `build.py`; no training images or derived image measurements are read by the builder.
