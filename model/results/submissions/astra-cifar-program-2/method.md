I drew the ten object families from geometric primitives: for example, cars have a glazed cabin and two axles, while a deer has a raised neck, narrow muzzle and slender legs. A deterministic renderer projects these models from multiple viewpoints. The classifier compares a photo's oriented edges, foreground mask and light/dark markings with those projections, testing different positions, sizes and reflections. It also scores complete scene hypotheses that distinguish the object's material from its surroundings, such as a hull above water or wheels above pavement. All reference arrays are generated from the geometry, hand-chosen material rules and fixed-seed procedural scenery in `build.py`; none contain training photos or fitted parameters.
