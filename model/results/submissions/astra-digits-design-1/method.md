I specified each digit as pen paths made of straight strokes, Bézier curves and ovals, including alternatives such as a looped 2, an open 4 and a crossed 7. A fixed-seed generator varies the slant, proportions, curvature and nib width, then scales and centers each drawing using the stated image convention. The weights compare blurred ink, pooled signed edges and unsigned stroke directions by squared distance. The classifier combines a whole-drawing match with independent matches for overlapping upper and lower parts, then averages the best matches from four groups of pen variations. A separate max-pooling flood fill measures the position and area of enclosed whitespace and uses an Euler count to distinguish two holes from one. All filters, paths and voting rules come from the builder; no training images, labels or fitted parameters are used to generate weights.
