The builder uses a fixed seed and hand-written garment patterns to draw silhouettes: it varies sleeve cuts, waist and hem widths, trouser legs, straps, soles, boot shafts, bag bodies, and handles. The classifier estimates a foreground mask for each photograph and compares its coarse occupancy and four directional contour profiles with the procedural shapes. Handwritten rules then look for details the outline alone cannot explain, such as a central placket or zip, knit waistband, all-over print, open sandal straps, or a bag handle. The shapes and rules were authored from garment geometry and qualitative visual inspection; no training images, descriptors, class averages, or fitted parameters are stored. Five disjoint labeled checks were run through the supplied checker. The final check classified 7,391 of 10,000 training images correctly; the hidden-set accuracy is unknown.
