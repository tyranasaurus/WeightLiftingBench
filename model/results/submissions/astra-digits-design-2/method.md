The digit models are pen paths I wrote on an arbitrary writing grid: for example, a 4 can have either a triangle or an open elbow, and a 2 can have a looped or a pointed foot. The builder sweeps a round pen along each path, varying its width, slant, proportions and curvature by fixed-seed formulas, then centers the impression by its ink mass. The network compares smoothed ink and signed directional edges against those analytic shapes, both as whole digits and as overlapping upper and lower parts with limited freedom to move. A grayscale flood fill measures enclosed loops and their locations after a soft morphological closing that bridges small pen gaps; max pooling and group consensus combine the evidence across writing styles. No training images, labels, learned coefficients, or external fonts are used to produce the weights.
