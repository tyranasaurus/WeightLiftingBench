The network is an analytic, multiscale template matcher. Ideal RGB versions of all 43 signs are drawn from circles, polygons, line segments, a hand-written digit font, and general-knowledge pictograms. Twelve poses vary scale, centre, aspect ratio, and rotation. A fixed bank produces RGB, colour-opponent, half-wave gradient, and Laplacian features; normalized correlations are evaluated on the whole image, an outer-sign crop, and an interior crop. Two tighter grayscale crops emphasize digits and symbols, while a colour-statistic layer supplies red/blue/yellow family evidence. Maximum pooling selects the best pose for each class, and the branch scores are added.

All weights and offsets are generated by `build.py` from those formulas and drawings. No training image, label statistic, fitted readout, regression, clustering, or gradient-based optimization is used. The submitted artifacts contain 24,989,912 float32 weights, below the 120,000,000-weight limit.
