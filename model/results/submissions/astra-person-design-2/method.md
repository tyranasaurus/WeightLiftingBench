The network looks for people at several scales. It tests for an oval of skin-like color, but also checks the left and right eye areas, nose bridge, cheeks, mouth, and hairline separately; each test has a capped vote so a single dark stripe cannot stand in for a whole face. Other branches look for a head over a contrasting shirt, a head-and-shoulders outline without a skin-color requirement, and oriented edges arranged like a standing, striding, throwing, or seated figure. The readout combines the strongest local matches with weaker evidence from the surrounding scene.

`build.py` constructs the weights from color inequalities, Gaussian and elliptical masks, and silhouettes drawn from ellipses and tapered limb segments. Coordinates describe generic anatomy, not specific photographs. No images, image-derived parameter estimates, learned weights, or external assets are used by the builder.
