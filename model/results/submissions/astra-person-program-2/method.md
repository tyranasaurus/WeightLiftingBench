The detector scans each photo at several scales and aspect ratios. It looks for a head above widening shoulders, checks for paired dark eye sockets and a mouth on larger faces, and tests short inside/outside contrast strips along standing or leaning body outlines. A separate search scores connected skin-colour regions for oval shape, hair above the region, and contrast with the surrounding scene. Repeated heads and cues such as grass beside infield clay provide supporting evidence.

I wrote the geometry, colour rules, and decision thresholds by hand. I inspected class contact sheets and used labeled sandbox runs as checks; no images, measured image features, or fitted weights are stored in the submission. `build.py` emits the complete NumPy implementation from its own source and does not need a constants file.
