I used a fixed hand-designed network with one 3x3 convolution layer, ReLU, 12x12 average pooling to an 8x8 grid, and a final dense classifier. The convolution layer contains generic luminance edge detectors in several orientations, a Laplacian-like texture detector, brightness and darkness channels, and a few simple color-opponent channels for blue, green, warm colors, and a rough skin-like color cue.

The dense layer is also hand-written. It gives positive weight to broadly distributed edge activity, some central skin-like evidence, darkness contrast, and blue/background structure, while giving negative weight to warm uniform regions and some border-heavy color evidence. All weights were typed from general image and person-scene priors rather than fitted from the training set.
