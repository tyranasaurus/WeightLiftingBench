The classifier compares each digit with procedural drawings, not examples from the training set. The builder defines the ten digits with Bézier strokes and arcs; a fixed seed varies the pen width, slant, curvature, and style choices such as an open or closed 4 or a looped 2. For each input, the classifier corrects slant, partly normalizes aspect ratio, and finds plausible drawings using spatially pooled edge directions. It then compares finer edge and ink patterns, allowing small local shifts, and checks the positions of enclosed spaces at several ink thresholds. The path definitions, transforms, and scoring rules were written by hand; no training image or label is an input to the builder.
