The network combines two analytic stroke-model scores. I typed idealized pen paths for common structures of each digit and generated deterministic fixed-seed variants in slant, rotation, curvature, aspect ratio, and stroke width. First, five Gaussian scales and eight oriented second-derivative bar filters are rectified and pooled into 4 by 4 cells; unit-normalized synthetic templates give cosine-similarity scores. Second, a deformable-part branch splits each of 80 variants per class into four arc-length segments, lets the segments translate independently, takes their minimum match, and then maximizes over variants. Small class offsets are hand-set. Every filter, pen path, variation, and threshold comes directly from `build.py`; no training image, label statistic, fitted readout, or learned weight is used.
