The network combines fixed Gaussian and directional-derivative features with a clipped foreground-silhouette branch. It compares both representations, by normalized dot product, against 192 deterministic parametric models per class. The models are drawn entirely from general clothing geometry: torso and sleeve polygons, neck and collar cuts, trouser legs, dress skirts, shoe soles and shafts, sandal straps, and bag bodies and handles. Subgroup maxima followed by averaging allow variation without depending on one exact outline.

A small third branch computes hand-set regional masses and spatial moments from the foreground mask. Its hand-written coefficients encode cues such as the trouser crotch gap, long sleeves, a dark coat/shirt opening, a filled dress hem, the wide low profile of sneakers and sandals, and a bag handle. All filters, model parameters, thresholds, and score offsets are constants or formulas in `build.py`; the training images and labels are not read and no fitting or optimization is used. The fixed random seed only varies the proportions and generic details of the synthetic geometric models.
