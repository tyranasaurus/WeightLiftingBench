The network combines two hand-designed views of morphology. First, normalized colour images are correlated with analytic, multi-scale templates: elliptical smooth profiles, exponential edge-on disks with and without bulges, barred and unbarred logarithmic spirals, irregular clumpy systems, and two-component mergers. A maximum over orientation and scale supplies rotation-tolerant template evidence. Second, fixed convolutions and dense spatial weights measure thresholded luminance moments, inner and outer ellipticity, concentration, odd asymmetry, colour, high-frequency structure, and logarithmic-spiral pitch. Rotated projections and ReLU pairs provide rotation-invariant magnitudes; clipped-ReLU circuits turn homogeneous moment comparisons into soft binary indicators for a fixed morphology rule table.

All weights are generated from formulas and hand-chosen dimensionless rules in `build.py`. The irregular and merger banks use fixed random seeds only to generate analytic shape parameters. No training image, label-derived statistic, fitted readout, or other external data is used by the build program.
