The network compresses brightness with a piecewise-linear approximation to a logarithm and uses red, blue and yellow color contrasts to locate sign frames. At each of 36 candidate positions and scales, it compares the face and its pictogram with hand-drawn geometry; a separate word reader checks the strokes and spacing of speed-limit numerals. The frame, legend and whole-sign branches cast scores for the same pose, and a max pool chooses the best pose for each class.

`build.py` constructs the interpolation matrices, analytic filters and individual sign templates from its source alone. Each template is normalized independently; there is no fitting, averaging across drawings, or extraction of weights from photographs. The fifth labeled check scored 79.48% on a fresh sample of 6,000 training photos, not on the hidden test set.
