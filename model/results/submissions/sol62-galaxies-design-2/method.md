The network subtracts a local sky estimate and builds surface-brightness, color, and fine-structure maps. Formula-generated Gaussian apertures, annular harmonics, opposed sectors, and logarithmic spiral filters measure ellipticity, asymmetry, arm winding, and the light above an edge-on disk. Center-surround and saddle filters look for a second nucleus. A dense/ReLU circuit combines this evidence into ten morphology scores using hand-written rules.

All weights come from these formulas and rules in `build.py`; the builder reads no data. I used the training images for visual inspection and five logged checks, not to fit or store weights. After the fifth check I changed the bulge readout to treat overlapping shape measurements as alternative evidence rather than adding them; the final readout was not run on labeled images.
