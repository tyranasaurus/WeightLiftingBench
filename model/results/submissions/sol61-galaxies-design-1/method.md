# Analytic galaxy morphology network

This is a fixed, data-free morphology network, not a trained classifier. It uses local sky subtraction, a piecewise-linear approximation to an asinh brightness transform, central-field contrast normalization, and banks of formula-generated seeing-blurred elliptical profiles, thin disks, disk-plus-bulge models, finite-width bars, and logarithmic spiral galaxies at multiple sizes, inclinations, angles, and winding directions. Additional branches measure rotational asymmetry, central versus off-center nuclear curvature, aperture colours, and inner versus outer quadrupole ellipticity; fixed, hand-written decision rules combine these into ten scores.

Every weight is generated in `build.py` from explicit formulas and general-purpose physical constants or hand-chosen thresholds. The builder reads no files and uses no training samples, patches, feature statistics, fitted parameters, pretrained weights, or randomness. Five permitted labeled diagnostic runs were made on the same fixed every-eighth-image subset (1,995 images); their accuracies were 31.58%, 37.09%, 34.84%, 33.78%, and 27.07%, with the outcomes and conceptual revisions reported during development. The submitted network includes a final unmeasured correction removing an extra smooth-disk penalty from the structured classes, dropping the unsuccessful radial upweighting, and restricting bar evidence to sufficiently elongated inner light; no claim is made for its accuracy or superiority to a trained network.
