The network is a hand-designed silhouette recognizer. Its hidden units correlate normalized multilevel versions of the input with families of analytic, idealized garment silhouettes (polygonal shirts, trousers, dresses, coats, shoes, bags, and boots), then use several ReLU hinge levels to approximate a best-match score. A second branch adds broad semantic part rules such as trouser leg separation, dress flare, low footwear extent, boot shafts, sandal sparsity, bag handles, sleeve length, and front openings.

Every weight is generated from typed geometric formulas and general knowledge of the ten clothing categories. No image, image measurement, descriptor, learned statistic, optimization, fitting, or training-derived number is used in the weights. The supplied training data was viewed only as a small class montage and with simple global format/intensity summaries, as permitted.
