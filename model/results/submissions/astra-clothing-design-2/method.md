The network compares a soft foreground mask and four directional outline maps with silhouettes drawn from parametric garment geometry. The drawings vary sleeve length, bodice and skirt proportions, trouser cut, upper and sole shape, sandal straps, and bag handles using a fixed seed; none comes from a photograph. A second pathway checks construction details, such as whether a dark slit has fabric on both sides all the way down the legs. Contrast-normalized filters look for center fastenings, lapels, curved necklines, and repeating woven patterns, and refine the scores within garment and shoe families. All weights are generated by the formulas and hand-written rules in `build.py`, without training or loading data.
