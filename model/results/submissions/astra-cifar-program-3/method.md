I built the classifier from hand-specified 3D drawings: for example, a car has a separate cabin, windshield and wheels, while a horse has a barrel-shaped body, long legs, a mane and an elongated muzzle. The data-free builder projects the drawings from several viewpoints and stores descriptors of their contours, silhouettes and internal shading. At prediction time the program compares those descriptors with each photo, then adds hand-written evidence for parts, coat patterns, texture and surroundings such as grass, sky, pavement and open water. No training images, image measurements, fitted parameters or pretrained weights are stored in the answer. Each image is scored independently, with fixed-size padded internal batches and no randomness.
