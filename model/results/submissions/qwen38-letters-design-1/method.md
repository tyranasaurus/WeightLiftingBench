# Method

## Architecture
A hand-designed, fully interpretable pipeline (13 layers, ~134k weights):

1. `norm` – per-image contrast/brightness normalisation (the photos vary in
   brightness/contrast and background level).
2. `c1` conv 9x9, 12 signed filters built from formulas: second-derivative-of-
   Gaussian ridge (bright-bar) detectors at 0/45/90/135 degrees in both
   polarities, a difference-of-Gaussians blob pair and a wide Gaussian pair.
3. `r1` relu, `c2` conv 1x1 – polarity-agnostic orientation energies
   (R0,R45,R90,R135) plus attenuated blob/mass energies (the hand is usually but
   not always lighter than the background, so signs are rectified and summed).
4. `p1` maxpool 2x2 -> 14x14.
5. `c3` conv 7x7 – integrates the energies into interpretable maps: FV/FH/FD1/FD2
   (elongated ridge integration = finger/stripe evidence per orientation), MASS
   (blurred blob energy), BODY (edge density), HOLE and HOLEW (narrow/wide
   centre-surround of edge density = valleys, inter-finger gaps, enclosed holes).
6. `r3` relu.
7. `reg` conv 14x14 – mean of each map over coarse boxes: a 3x3 spatial grid per
   channel plus finer bands (top-strip column bands of stripe/gap energy,
   bottom-strip stripe bands for downward-pointing fingers, right-side row bands
   of horizontal-stripe/gap energy) -> ~71 position features.
8. `patc` conv 7x7 + `patm` maxpool 8x8 – sliding configuration detectors
   (1/2/3/4 vertical stripes, 1/2 horizontal stripes, enclosed ring, crossed
   diagonals, hook, L-corner); the max over slide positions means "pattern
   present anywhere" -> 10 more features.
9. `feat` add + `fv` reshape -> one 81-dim interpretable feature vector.
10. `sc` dense 24 – a hand-written rule table: each class score is a signed
    weighted sum of the features that its hand shape should show (e.g. V = two
    top stripe bands + wide top gaps; U = two top stripe bands and *no* wide
    gap; G = horizontal stripe row-bands on the right + mass on the left;
    Q = stripes in the bottom strip + mass on top; O = enclosed hole +
    curvature; the fist family = high central mass + knuckle-stripe count),
    with negative weights on features that rival letters show.

## How the weights were produced
Every number is generated by `build.py` from closed-form filter formulas
(Gaussian derivatives, differences of Gaussians, centre-surround) and from a
typed shape model of the 24 ASL letters (thumb side = viewer's right, pinky side
= viewer's left, which letters show holes/hooks/crossed or downward fingers, how
many finger stripes each fist shows). The rule-table weights and the feature
scale constants were chosen by hand; the only measurements used were of feature
values on synthetic bar/blob drawings of the letters that I typed myself (no
training image was ever used to compute any weight). No gradient descent, no
fitting, no stored images or templates from images.

## Validation
Three of the five allowed checks were run on a stratified 6000-image subset of
the training set (best: 12.9% accuracy), plus synthetic-drawing unit tests of the
whole network. The confusion shows the geometrically distinctive letters
(C, G, H, L, Y, W, D, O, Q) are partially separated while the fist family
(A, S, E, M, N) and single-finger letters still collapse into C/D/F; the rule
table needs sharper fist-family and gap-width features to go further.
