The classifier matches the image to a procedural anatomical model of each of the 24 letters. The model uses a tapered palm, a wrist, and chains of rounded finger segments; a deterministic sequence varies articulation, projection, framing, proportions, and lighting. One network branch measures oriented transitions, a second measures finger-width ridges and grooves, and a third measures coarse shading after discounting a horizontal background gradient. The readout combines two votes: one compares the complete hand, while the other compares edge and ridge patterns after normalizing contrast within nine smaller regions. Each vote searches over translations and averages the best matches from four groups of poses. All weights are built from the geometry and filter formulas in `build.py`; no training images, fitted readout, or learned parameters are used.
