The network compares each photo's normalized luminance, oriented edges, and opponent colors to drawings made from ellipses, polygons, and line segments. Each class has 960 fixed-seed variations of hand-specified poses; the vehicle drawings include perspective projections of bodies, windows, and wheels. A second branch tests parts and settings: for example, two tires above pavement, eyes above a muzzle, or a long hull separating sky and water. Color palettes and texture filters help distinguish materials such as grass, fur, and painted metal. The build script generates every weight from these formulas and hand-written rules, without reading photos, labels, or external assets and without fitting parameters.
