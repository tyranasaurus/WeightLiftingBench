The classifier subtracts the local sky background, locates the central galaxy, and traces contours of equal brightness. It uses those contours to measure apparent axis ratio, isophote asymmetry, colour changes, and the projected disk orientation. In deprojected log-polar coordinates it searches for bars and spiral arms using angular Fourier harmonics, analytic logarithmic spirals, and local ridge angles; separate rules look for pairs of resolved nuclei and for a bulge that thickens an edge-on disk. The rules and filters were written by hand from morphology and image-processing considerations, with visual inspection of labeled contact sheets and five sandbox checks on disjoint training subsets. No images, learned parameters, or feature tables are stored; the self-contained builder emits the classifier source directly.
