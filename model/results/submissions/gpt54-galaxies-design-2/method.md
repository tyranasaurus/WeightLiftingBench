This answer uses a fixed two-scale template-matching network built only from hand-written analytic filters. The image is average-pooled to 32×32 and 16×16, converted to a luminance-like channel plus a red-minus-blue colour channel, then matched against banks of galaxy-shape templates: round smooth profiles, ellipses, cigar shapes, edge-on discs with and without a bulge, two-core mergers, lopsided disturbed systems, and logarithmic-spiral patterns for tight, loose, and barred spirals. The template responses are rectified and pooled so each morphology group contributes a strongest-match score at each scale.

The final classifier is a hand-chosen linear readout from those pooled morphology scores to the 10 classes. All weights come from formulas and manually chosen constants in build.py; nothing is fitted from the training set.
