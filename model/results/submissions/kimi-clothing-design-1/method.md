The network is a hand-designed, multi-resolution template matcher. I generated 32 analytic drawings per class from general clothing geometry: parameterized tops, trousers with two legs, dresses with waist/skirt variants, coats and shirts with lapels or plackets, bags with bodies and handles, and mirrored sandals, sneakers, and boots with different soles and heels. Every drawing is centred and isotropically scaled by its own bounding box, antialiased, blurred, and normalized to zero mean and unit length; no training pixels or fitted statistics are used.

Three convolution branches compare the input with these templates: 14x14 average-pooled shape matching, 28x28 intensity/detail matching, and 14x14 centre-surround contour matching. Reshaping followed by one-dimensional max pooling selects the best variant within each class. A final hand-set linear rule layer adds evidence for a gap between trouser legs, long sleeves, a centre-front opening, and a flared skirt. Dense diagonal layers set the relative contributions of the three matching branches, and their outputs are summed into the ten class scores.

`build.py` constructs every weight from these formulas and writes both required files without reading any data. The selected version achieved 0.5258 accuracy on the 60,000 training images in the fourth permitted end-to-end check; the fifth experimental revision was rejected, and this selected version passed the standalone rebuild check with zero numerical difference.
