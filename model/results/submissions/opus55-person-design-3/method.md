# Method

**Architecture** (about 60 layers, 1.29 M weight entries, about 0.35 M of them non-zero). All weights are written by hand in `build.py`.

1. **Colour stage (1x1 convolutions, relu).**
   - Each channel goes through a piecewise-linear natural log (hinges at knots a factor of about 1.5 apart). Colour ratios then become differences: u = ln(R/G) and v = ln(G/B).
   - A partial grey-world white balance is applied. The photo's mean u and v are global-average-pooled, then broadcast back to every pixel by a dense + reshape layer. That shifts every pixel's u, v by -0.5 x (mean - 0.05).
   - Fuzzy membership maps are built from clipped ramps, clip(a, 0, 1) = relu(a) - relu(a-1), combined with a Łukasiewicz AND, relu(sum - (n-1)):
     - **skin core**: R/G about 1.2–1.6, G > B, G/B up to about 1.35, ln(G/B) up to 0.8·ln(R/G) (skin is pinker than wood, bread, sand, tan fur, dirt or bark), and R bright enough;
     - **warm-but-not-skin**: wood, tan, beige, fur, bread;
     - **dark**: hair, shadow;
     - **chroma**: zero for black-and-white photos;
     - **bluish snow-white** and **saturated yellow/orange**: both measured on the un-balanced colours;
     - a constant "inside the photo" channel, which is 0 in zero padding.
2. **Head / skin-blob detectors at six scales** (cells of 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 px, average-pooled at half-cell stride, then a 5x5 conv whose taps compare neighbouring cells):
   - **blob** = AND(cell mostly skin; left, right and above mostly neutral, i.e. neither skin nor warm). Corners and edges of large skin-coloured areas fail this test. So do pinkish spots embedded in wood, fur or food.
   - **hair head** = AND(cell mostly skin; left and right not skin; cell above partly dark).
   - Outside the photo counts as 80% non-skin. Detections are counted per scale with global average pooling.
3. **Hand-set evidence sum** (dense → relu → dense; each term is capped):
   - **+ terms**: skin fraction (per 0.4% of the photo), compact-blob count, hair-head count, snow covering the lower two-thirds (skiing scenes), black-and-white photo (a mild person prior).
   - **− terms**: skin colour over more than a quarter of the photo (walls, tables), a large share of warm non-skin colour, a large share of saturated yellow/orange (food close-ups, wood, giraffes).
   - Output 1 minus output 0 is this sum; output 0 is a constant 0.

**How the numbers were chosen.** Colour loci come from general knowledge of skin chromaticity: Macbeth "light/dark skin" patches, the normalised-rg skin locus, and typical colours of wood, bread, fur and sand. Templates come from a "face = skin oval with hair above, background at the sides" model. Weights and thresholds are hand-chosen log-odds-style values. Nothing is fitted, and no statistic of the training images or labels went into any weight.

I looked at about 250 training photos as PNG grids, only to understand the classes. Before each check, the network was unit-tested only on synthetic patterns generated by formula: colour swatches of reference colours, drawn faces with hair, uniform fields, and a snow scene. It was never run on training images outside the logged checks.

**The 5 checks, each on all 20,000 training images:**

| Check | Design | Accuracy | Person recall | False alarms on no-person |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loose skin rule, any single blob | 52.1% | 90% | 84% |
| 2 | Tight skin core, white balance, aggregated evidence | 58.1% | 70% | 53% |
| 3 | + neutral surroundings, hair heads, snow and yellow cues | 60.4% | 61% | 40% |
| 4 | + skin hue wedge, looser face-cell purity, more weight on hair | 58.6% | 60% | 42% |
| 5 | Check-3 design with stricter face-cell purity | 60.2% | 52% | 32% |

What each check taught:
- **Check 1:** compact skin-coloured spots are in nearly every photo, and taking the maximum over thousands of positions amplifies false alarms.
- **Check 2:** tighter colour and aggregated evidence helped, but skin amount alone is weak.
- **Check 3:** demanding neutral surroundings gave the best separation.
- **Check 4:** loosening per-position criteria hurt.
- **Check 5:** purity only moves the operating point along the same ROC curve.

The submitted network is the check-3 design, restored exactly; its weights are identical to the ones that were checked.
