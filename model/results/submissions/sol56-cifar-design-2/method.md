# Method

This is a fully data-free analytic network; no optimization, fitting, dataset statistics, or image-derived numbers are used in its weights.

The first branch applies 32 hand-written RGB half-space tests for brightness and broad colour categories. The second applies 64 signed Gaussian-derivative and elongated-ridge filters at eight orientations and two scales, followed by per-image normalization. Both branches pool to an 8 x 8 grid.

A bank of 1,000 hypotheses (100 per class) correlates those grids with generic geometric object models: for example, airplanes use fuselage/wing/tail line arrangements, automobiles and trucks use boxes and circular wheels, ships use hull/deck/mast lines, and animals use ellipses plus class-appropriate heads, ears, legs, necks, wings, or splayed limbs. The hypotheses cover hand-selected flips, small translations, scales, rotations, and poses. Their maximum is combined with weak common-sense scene-colour evidence (sky/water for ships, sky for airplanes, vegetation for deer/frogs/horses) and broad orientation summaries. All templates and constants are generated by formulas and general class knowledge directly in `build.py`.
