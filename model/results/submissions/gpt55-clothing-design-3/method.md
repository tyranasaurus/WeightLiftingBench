Hand-written classifier using data-free geometric templates and semantic region rules. The input is channel-normalised, correlated with individual formula-drawn clothing, shoe and bag silhouettes, rectified, and averaged by class; additional ReLU features encode broad cues such as low shoes, trouser leg gaps, bag handles, long sleeves and skirt-like solid lower regions. All weights are generated from analytic shapes and hand-chosen constants in build.py; no training images or labels are read when producing the submitted weights.
