I used a fully hand-written convolutional network.  The first convolution contains formulaic colour-opponent, brightness, Gaussian-smoothed colour, Sobel/Laplacian, and Gabor-like edge filters.  The second convolution combines those into generic hand-coded part cues such as wheels, small dark blobs, horizontal bodies, vertical legs, boxy parts, wing-like diagonals, hulls, green/brown blobs, and furry texture; all masks are analytic disks, bars, crosses, and diagonals written in code.

The pooled part maps feed a small hand-authored rule layer.  Each rule is a generic scene/object conjunction such as sky plus diagonal wings for airplane, low wheels plus horizontal body for automobile/truck, water plus hull for ship, close furry central texture for cat/dog, green rounded blobs for frog, and brown bodies with legs in grass for deer/horse.  The final dense layer combines these rules with manually chosen class scales and small priors.  No training, fitting, class averages, image templates, or weights derived from training images were used; the training set was only viewed in montages and used for the five allowed end-to-end checks.
