I used a fixed, hand-written template matcher rather than any fitted parameters. The network first averages each 2x2 block to 16x16, then scores every class with two dense branches: one branch matches pooled RGB values against synthetic sign templates, and the other matches channel-normalized pooled images against normalized versions of the same templates.

The templates were drawn in code from general knowledge of German road-sign shapes and colours: red-ring speed signs with bitmap digits, warning triangles with simple black symbols, yellow priority diamonds, blue mandatory discs with white arrows, and the cancellation signs with diagonal stripes. All weights come directly from those procedural templates in build.py; no training, fitting, class statistics, or copied image patches were used.
