I saved a hand-built template-matching network. It first averages the 128×128 image down to 16×16 and 32×32 feature maps, then applies convolutional banks whose weights are generated from analytic galaxy-shape templates: smooth round/elliptical/cigar profiles, edge-on disks with and without bulges, spiral-arm patterns with different pitch angles, bar detectors, and local disturbed/merger detectors. Each bank is reduced with max pooling over template variants, and small dense readouts combine those pooled feature scores into the 10 class logits.

All weights were written directly from code in `build.py`. I produced them by typing formulas for ellipses, rings, and logarithmic-spiral-like patterns, choosing scales, colors, and class-combination coefficients by hand from general knowledge of the galaxy classes and by visually inspecting training examples, without gradient descent or fitting the training set into weights.
