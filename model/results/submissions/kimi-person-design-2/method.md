The network is an entirely hand-designed, multi-scale person detector. A four-level area-average image pyramid feeds triangular YCbCr skin-colour responses, Gaussian centre-surround head templates, and face templates with explicitly paired dark eye regions and weaker mouth support. At two coarser scales, normalized Sobel gradients are matched against three generic humanoid silhouettes drawn analytically from circles and capsules (standing, walking, and raised arms). Body scores fuse silhouette evidence with skin evidence at the expected head location. Valid convolutions ensure that every signed template is evaluated only where it fits completely.

Global max pooling aggregates detections at any position, and a small hand-set readout combines the face, body, isolated-head, and silhouette cues. All weights come from colour-space formulas, Gaussian/Sobel kernels, geometric drawing code, and manually chosen constants in `build.py`; no training images, labels, fitted statistics, or optimization were used. The five permitted checks were used to correct border handling, evidence conjunctions, and the decision threshold. The final logged check on the 20,000-image development sample achieved 54.32% accuracy.
