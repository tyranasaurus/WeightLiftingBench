The builder defines a three-dimensional palm, forearm, four articulated fingers and an opposable thumb in palm-width units. Each letter specifies which joints flex, which fingers touch or spread, and how the hand is presented to the viewer. A fixed-seed renderer varies articulation, proportions, camera angle and light; the constants describe only these synthetic hands. The predictor estimates foreground evidence and searches over crop position and scale using local edge orientations. It then checks shortlisted poses using shading, enclosed gaps, and finger contours, allowing small local shifts while penalizing fingers present on only one side of the comparison. Labeled photos were viewed to understand pose conventions and official checks were used for diagnostics; no photo measurements, training descriptors, or fitted classifier weights enter the builder.
