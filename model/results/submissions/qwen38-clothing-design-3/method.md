# Method

## Architecture

The network is a hand-engineered feature pipeline whose every number comes from
formulas and from general knowledge of what clothing, shoes and bags look like in
a shop photo. No data is read, nothing is fitted, and no statistic of the training
set enters any weight.

**Base maps.** A saturated *presence* map (`relu(5x-0.3) - relu(5x-1.3)`) turns any
item pixel into ~1 regardless of brightness, so dark uppers and bright soles count
equally; a Gaussian-smoothed brightness map is kept separately for internal cues
(seams, plackets, texture). Both are contrast-normalised with `norm`.

**Template branch (the core).** Ten silhouette models are drawn part by part from
general knowledge — tee/polo/shirt/pullover/coat/dress as body + sleeve polygons
with neck notches and lapels, trouser as waist + two legs with a crotch gap,
sandals as sole line + straps + heel post, sneakers as sole bar + wedge upper,
boots as foot polygon + ankle shaft (both orientations), bags as tote/clutch/
satchel/duffel bodies with handle arcs. Each drawing is rasterised at 4x
supersampling, blurred, and turned into a zero-mean unit-norm filter, i.e. a
normalised-cross-correlation template. Templates are matched at three scales
(28x28 occupancy, 28x28 difference-of-Gaussians outline, 14x14 and 7x7 occupancy)
by dense layers; per class the maximum over its 8 variant templates is taken with
reshape + maxpool, which makes the match tolerant to cut, pose and orientation.

**Structural rule branch.** Fixed filters compute interpretable measurements:
row/column mass profiles; band widths and row/column extents from thresholded
presence; sleeve length from outer-column mass in the lower half; aspect from
extent counts; the trouser crotch gap and open sandal structure via morphological
opening deficits and closing hole-fills (built from maxpool + sign-flip convs)
split into top/bottom halves; |Laplacian| texture globally, in the centre column
band and in the bottom band (sole edge); left/right asymmetry (boot shaft); and a
centre-line seam detector (coat/shirt front opening). A hand-written rule table
(per-class weights, all typed by hand) turns each measurement into class bonuses.

**Readout.** The per-class template maxima and all rule contributions are summed
with `add` into the final 10 scores. Total: ~146k weights.

## How the weights were produced

All weights are generated by `build.py` from code alone: template filters from the
parametric silhouette drawings (rectangles, trapezoids, ellipses, arcs, thick
lines) via closed-form rasterisation and normalisation; fixed analytic filters
(Gaussian, DoG, Laplacian, sign flips, centre-one crops); pooling/reshape wiring;
and the hand-chosen rule table. Thresholds and rule weights were set by hand and
revised by reasoning about the *classes* (e.g. "a dark sneaker with a bright sole
must not look like a sandal", "sleeve length lives in the outer columns"), using
rendered views of training images for qualitative understanding and synthetic
degraded renderings of my own silhouette models as wiring unit tests. The five
allowed runs on real training images were used only to confirm the network works
and to expose mis-calibrations (final such run: 54.2% on 6000 images).
