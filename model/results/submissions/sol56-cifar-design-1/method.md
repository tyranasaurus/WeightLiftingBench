This submission is a fully analytic, data-free convolutional classifier. A fixed first stage computes luminance, darkness, opponent-colour, and threshold-like colour channels. A second stage applies Gaussian-derivative, Gabor-like, and centre-surround filters generated from formulas at several scales and orientations.

The resulting edge maps feed hand-drawn geometric prototypes for wings, fuselages, wheels, hulls, masts, faces, ears, quadruped bodies and legs, and a frog-like compact outline. Template responses are translation-tolerant through max pooling. A parallel branch pools coarse colour layout (for cues such as sky, water, vegetation, road darkness, and object colour), and a hand-authored semantic rule table combines both branches into ten scores.

No optimizer, regression, clustering, class average, image statistic, image-derived template, or pretrained parameter was used. All weights are reproducibly generated by formulas and general object knowledge in `build.py`. Five balanced checks were used only to diagnose execution and broad score calibration; their final measured accuracy was 15.8% on the fixed 500-image check subset.
