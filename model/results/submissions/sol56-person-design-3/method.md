This is a data-free, hand-designed multiscale detector. It converts normalized luminance into four unsigned Gaussian-derivative/Sobel orientation maps and computes three broad, hinge-based skin-colour maps plus coarse luminance. A bank of analytically drawn HOG-like templates represents generic full figures, walking/action poses, head-and-shoulder silhouettes, and frontal/profile faces at eight scales; spatial and template maxima are combined into the class scores.

All coefficients come from standard luminance/Sobel formulas, generic geometric body models, broad colour inequalities, and hand-selected detector margins. No training image, label, crop, descriptor, aggregate statistic, optimization, fitting, or external file is used to produce any weight. `build.py` deterministically generates both the architecture and every weight from those formulas.
