The network uses hand-written RGB opponent-color inequalities and ReLUs to produce soft skin-color and luminance maps. Six sizes of Gaussian spatial templates reward an oval skin region with darker eye and mouth bands and contrast against its surroundings. The readout combines the strongest response at each size, average template responses, and overall skin-color coverage. The Gaussian geometry, color inequalities, and readout coefficients come from general image and face heuristics; no training examples are encoded in the weights and no parameters were fitted. Labeled checks were only used to inspect behavior; the build script reads no data or other files.
