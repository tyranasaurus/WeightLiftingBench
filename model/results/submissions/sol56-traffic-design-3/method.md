The network is an analytic template matcher rather than a fitted model. Its first convolution is a hand-constructed bank of Gaussian-smoothed colour-opponent, luminance-gradient, and Laplacian filters; per-image channel normalization makes those features robust to lighting, while the Gaussian smoothing reduces sensitivity to jagged nearest-neighbour resizing.

`build.py` draws each German sign class from general sign semantics using circles, triangles, polygons, strokes, arrows, standard digits, and symbolic pictograms. It renders a fixed bank of generic scale, aspect, translation, rotation, and stroke-width variants, transforms those drawings through the same analytic feature bank, and writes their normalized feature maps as dense correlation weights. A max pool selects the best geometric variant for each class. No training image, label-derived statistic, optimization, regression, clustering, or fitted parameter is used.
