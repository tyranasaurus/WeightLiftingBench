The network computes spatially normalized luminance and rectified red, blue, and yellow opponent-color maps, then averages them over 2×2 cells. A dense layer matches those features against analytic drawings of all 43 signs, covering 180 combinations of scale, translation, rotation, and aspect ratio per class. Max pooling takes the best match for each class. All weights come from formulas and hand-drawn geometric primitives in build.py; no training images or fitted statistics are used. One labeled check on 500 randomly sampled training photos yielded 51.8% accuracy; held-out accuracy is unknown.
