The network is a hand-designed multi-scale template and scene-rule classifier. A fixed bank of eight Gaussian-derivative filters supplies oriented edges; these and normalized RGB are pooled at 16×16 and 8×8 resolutions. The template weights come from 960 independent programmatic drawings of generic class members in four viewpoints, several scales, shifts, and angles, plus 120 local object drawings. Each drawing is transformed by the same fixed feature formulas and individually centered and normalized; no training image or cross-image statistic is used. Class-wise maxima provide viewpoint tolerance, with background-only drawing residuals and hand-written spatial-colour, colour-tail, and oriented-edge rules supplying additional evidence. Final class biases were calibrated only from the aggregate confusion tables of the five permitted checks. The final labeled training-set check reached 17.83% accuracy. `build.py` generates all architecture entries and weights from formulas and fixed drawing code without reading any files.
