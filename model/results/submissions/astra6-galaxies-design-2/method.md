# Analytic galaxy morphology network

The network uses fixed sky subtraction, central spatial windows, a square-root intensity transform, and tolerant half-turn symmetry to emphasize the central galaxy. Dense matched-filter banks are generated from analytic Sersic ellipses, exponential disks with optional round bulges, finite Ferrers bars, logarithmic spiral arms, and two-nucleus models over prescribed scales and orientations. Max pooling provides approximate scale and rotation invariance; additional fixed aperture measurements describe colour, ellipticity, texture, and asymmetry. ReLU rules combine these quantities into ten class scores, with a bar required to outperform smooth and edge-on alternatives.

Every weight is produced by the formulas and hand-written rules in `build.py`, which reads no data or other files. No optimization, statistical parameter estimation, pretrained weights, image-derived templates, or stored training descriptors are used. The build writes float32 weights using `np.savez` and the allowed-operation architecture using JSON.

Exactly five labeled checker runs were made on the same fixed, label-independent random subset of 1,000 training images. Their accuracies were 30.7%, 18.2%, 37.7%, 34.1%, and 32.9%; these were development checks, not held-out performance measurements. The final check revealed that the bar comparison omitted narrow smooth alternatives. The submitted architecture corrects this by using the maximum of all existing smooth/nonbarred template-bank scores as the bar null hypothesis. This final structural correction has not been evaluated on images because the five-check allowance was exhausted; no claim of beating either comparator is made.
