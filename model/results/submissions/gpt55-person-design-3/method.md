I built a fixed, data-free convolutional network with hand-coded filters and templates. The first layers compute simple colour, brightness, edge, saturation, skin-colour, hair/dark, and foreground maps from formulaic RGB and derivative filters. Later layers use manually specified oval face, head-and-shoulder, upper-body, and standing-body part templates at several sizes; small 1x1 conjunction layers require compatible parts before max-pooling over image locations. The final score is a conservative sum of these hand-designed person evidences plus a weak global skin/foreground prior. All weights are generated procedurally in build.py from general geometric and colour assumptions, not from fitted statistics or copied training images.
