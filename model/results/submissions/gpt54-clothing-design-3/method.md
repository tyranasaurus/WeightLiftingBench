This network uses three fixed template branches on downsampled versions of the image. One branch uses 7x7 average pooling, one uses 14x14 max pooling, and one uses 14x14 average pooling. Each branch first converts every pooled pixel into two thresholded features: occupied and empty. A second dense layer scores hand-written shape templates made from coarse rectangles for trousers, dresses, tops, pullovers, coats, shirts, bags, sneakers, sandals, and boots. A final dense layer adds class votes from the activated templates, and the three branches are summed.

I chose the templates and thresholds manually from general knowledge about the class silhouettes: split trouser legs, flared dress bottoms, bag bodies with handles, low shoes near the bottom edge, open sandals with gaps, and boots with taller ankles. No training, fitting, image statistics, or copied image patches were used; every weight is produced directly from constants in build.py.
