The network converts grayscale intensities into soft foreground occupancy and lightly blurs the result. A dense layer correlates that map against 960 procedural silhouettes per class; max pooling retains the strongest match for each class. The silhouettes are drawn from hand-written geometric models such as sleeves and necklines, pant legs, sandal straps, and bag handles, with fixed-seed variation in proportions and alignment. Templates are centered and normalized using only their own generated pixels. The builder reads no data; three labeled checks were used to diagnose recognition errors, not to fit the weights.
