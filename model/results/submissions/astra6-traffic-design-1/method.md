# Analytic traffic-sign network

This is a fixed, hand-designed network, not a trained model. It uses luminance and bounded opponent-colour channels, formula-generated ring/triangle/diamond masks, and 216 fixed scale/aspect/position/rotation hypotheses. Separate symbol branches sample sign interiors by fixed bilinear matrices, normalize their contrast, and correlate them with mathematical drawings of numerals, arrows, vehicles, warning pictograms, and cancellation strokes. The drawings use line segments, ellipses and polygons in abstract sign coordinates, with fixed antialiasing quadrature and 96 shared drafting variations; max pooling selects a hypothesis for each class. Explicit rules test blank interiors and prevent contradictory colour-family/polarity interpretations.

Every weight is produced directly by the formulas and manually specified general sign geometry in `build.py`. The builder reads no files, uses no external assets or fonts, and performs no training, fitting, optimization, or data-dependent parameter calculation. Training images were viewed as permitted; five labelled checks were used to diagnose implementation and representation limitations, without extracting image coordinates, pixels, descriptors, class averages, or fitted constants into the weights. The check sample contains 24 images per class and is not the hidden evaluation set.

Reproduce the answer with `python3 build.py NEW_FOLDER`. The final network has 87 layers and 106,231,207 float32 weight entries, below the 120,000,000-entry limit. The five permitted checks reported accuracies of 38.95%, 47.00%, 48.74%, 55.23%, and 57.66% on the same 1,032-image balanced sample. The last result describes the submitted network, not held-out accuracy. Performance remains limited, especially on detailed warning symbols and difficult speed-limit images; no claim is made that it beats either unseen comparison system.
