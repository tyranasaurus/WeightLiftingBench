The classifier matches a photograph against hand models built from an elliptical palm, a wrist, and tapered, shaded finger segments. Each letter has a hand-authored articulation; a fixed seed varies the camera and finger geometry to produce 128 poses, each tested at nine translations. The network compares oriented edges, estimated foreground, spaces between digits, and silhouettes viewed along rows and columns, while allowing the knuckles and fingertips to align separately. Max and average pooling combine these comparisons into the 24 class scores. The builder computes all weights from the geometry and analytic filters without reading photographs or labels or fitting any parameters.

The final labeled check classified 38.38% of a fresh 6,000-image training subset correctly. This is not a held-out test result. The network shape check passed (94,386,068 weights), and the isolated rebuild matched the submitted architecture and weights with zero reported numerical difference.
