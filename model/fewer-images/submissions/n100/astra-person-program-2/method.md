The detector scans several head sizes for agreement between skin-colored regions, face structure, a head-and-shoulder outline, and a wider clothed torso. It also compares oriented edges with individual hand-drawn figure templates and checks whether separated skin-colored regions could be a face and hands. Evidence from the strongest spatially distinct candidates is combined with weaker scene cues, such as long straight edges or a figure against snow or grass.

The build script draws each contour from anatomical proportions, computes its orientation filter, and normalizes that filter using only the same drawing. The geometry, color rules, and evidence-combination rules are handwritten; no weights or templates were fitted to photos. Three labeled development checks were run. The final program classified 112 of the 200 examples correctly (68 people and 44 no-person photos); this is not a held-out evaluation.
