The network separates overall shape from construction details. Its shape branch compares a softened foreground map to 10,240 individual templates drawn from hand-written patterns for bodices, sleeves, skirts, trouser legs, footwear and bags. A fixed seed varies their proportions; each drawing is framed, centered in intensity and scaled using only its own pixels, then used as one dense filter. No training image supplies a template or a weight.

Other branches use hand-specified filters and ReLU rules to detect details such as gaps between straps, continuous shoe sidewalls, separated legs, collar shapes, sleeve lengths, plackets and fabric patterns. Details rank labels within a family without displacing the family's overall shape score. The fifth and final labeled check was 726/1,000 correct; this is a development check, not a held-out result.
