`build.py` draws each class from a hand-written shape grammar: for example, it projects vehicle bodies, windows and wheels at different angles, and constructs animals from articulated limbs, heads and markings. A fixed random seed varies pose, scale, material, lighting and plausible backgrounds. Each drawing contributes one template with three descriptions: multiscale contours and contrast, regional color, and local shape on the drawn object's support. These are normalized per drawing; no templates are averaged and no parameters are fitted. `predict` compares each input with the individual templates, then combines the strongest matches with hand-written cues for features such as wheel pairs, eyes and muzzles, legs, sky, vegetation and water.
