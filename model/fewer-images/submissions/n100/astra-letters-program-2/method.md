I specified an articulated hand in anatomical units, with a padded palm, tapered finger segments and an opposable thumb. The builder poses it for each sign and renders 1,280 templates per letter, varying joint angles, viewpoint, wrist position, crop, lighting and background. Each stored row describes one render, normalized independently. For a query, the classifier compares oriented gradients, shading, ridge responses and estimated foreground coverage across translations, then allows small local displacements around the best matches. I inspected labeled photos for viewing conventions and used the logged checks to identify failure modes; no training photos, measurements from them, fitted weights or class averages enter the build.
