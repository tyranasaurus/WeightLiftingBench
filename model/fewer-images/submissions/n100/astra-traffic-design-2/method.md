The network scores each sign's outline and its contents at the same proposed position, size, and tilt before max-pooling over those alternatives. A piecewise-linear exposure transform and red/blue/yellow opponent channels help identify the sign face; luminance and rectified dark-stroke channels read its contents. For red-bordered and cancellation signs, a second reader normalizes contrast using only pixels inside each candidate face, excluding the cancellation stripe where applicable. Speed limits also have a character reader that checks the distinguishing digit against the neighboring numeral.

`build.py` draws the outlines, numerals, vehicles, arrows, and warning symbols from analytic geometry and hand-specified strokes. Each matched filter is normalized from its own drawing only. The remaining weights implement fixed color transforms, sampling, normalization support, and score routing; no weights were fitted and the build reads no image data.
