I built a bank of 3-D object models from simple solids and polygons: for example, the car has a separate cabin, windows, and wheels, the horse has articulated legs and a mane, and the frog has a low body, folded hind legs, and raised eyes. A fixed sequence of poses, scales, materials, and lights produces individual grayscale views. Each view independently supplies one matched filter with luminance, center–surround, and orientation-selective contour components; no drawing contributes to any other filter or to the choice of gains. The network pools pose matches within each class and adds evidence from bilateral symmetry, straight edges, and hand-written rules for scene layout, such as water below a hull, vegetation around a grazer, or a dark wheel band over gray pavement. All weight generation is data-free, with no fitted readout or training-photo templates.
