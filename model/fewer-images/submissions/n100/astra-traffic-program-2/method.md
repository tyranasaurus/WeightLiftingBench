The classifier first estimates a sign's family, center, size, and shear by comparing color and brightness on the two sides of a circular, triangular, diamond, or octagonal boundary. It then samples the sign in normalized coordinates and compares the interior with hand-drawn vector pictograms at several widths, heights, and offsets. Speed limits get a second reading from a glyph-by-glyph recognizer; separate tests look for a continuous no-entry bar, an empty disc, and the diagonal band on a canceled restriction.

`build.py` draws every template from geometric primitives and normalizes each template independently. It uses no external assets or training data. The supplied photos were used for visual inspection and the official labeled checks, not for fitting or extracting templates.
