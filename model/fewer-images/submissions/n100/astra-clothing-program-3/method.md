The classifier compares each silhouette to a catalogue drawn from hand-specified garment patterns, shoe profiles, and bag bodies and handles. The builder samples dimensions such as sleeve length, chest width, heel height, and strap position without reading any files; each rendered pattern is framed and described independently. At prediction time it compares foreground occupancy, aspect ratio, and scan-line margins, then uses hand-written cues for details that silhouettes miss, including a front closure, lapels, neckline, patterned fabric, trouser-leg gaps, and spaces between straps. The provided photographs were used for visual inspection and labeled sanity checks, not to fit weights, extract templates, or set constants from measured training statistics.
