The detector scans the photo at several scales and aspect ratios. It looks for a bounded region of skin above clothing, compares local brightness patterns with face sketches, and compares edge orientations with drawn head-and-shoulders and full-body contours. A separate silhouette test looks for a narrow head, wider torso, and legs against a locally consistent background. The detector compares upright matches with upside-down or sideways controls in the same photo, so that a high score from background texture counts for less. It also checks for a similarly colored hand beside the torso and uses weaker scene cues, including long straight edges and whether detail concentrates near the center.

I wrote the geometry, color rules, and decision thresholds by hand. `build.py` constructs and normalizes each contour or face template independently from its own formula. It reads no images, labels, pretrained weights, or other data files.
