The classifier searches for a sign face at several positions, sizes, aspect ratios, and shears, using luminance and opponent-color contrasts across the expected rim and interior. It rectifies that face and compares the ink inside it with individually normalized drawings of road lettering, vehicles, and warning symbols. It reads speed limits both as whole strings and as separately aligned characters, checking for enclosed light regions that distinguish an 8 from an open digit. Shape-specific tests also look for a continuous cancellation stripe, the vertical color order of traffic lights, and the three arrows of a roundabout. `build.py` constructs the templates from polygons, strokes, curves, and geometric formulas in its source; it reads no training images, fonts, or other assets. Prediction uses only fixed filters and per-photo measurements, without training or batch statistics.
