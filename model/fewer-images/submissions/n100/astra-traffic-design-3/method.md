I drew ideal sign faces in code from circles, polygons, strokes, and Bézier curves, including the speed-limit numerals. Each pose of each drawing gives its own correlation filters; the filters are centered and contrast-normalized on that individual template, with separate masks for the whole face and its interior. The network compresses camera intensities, separates brightness and color contrast, and measures fine ink detail with a difference of Gaussians. A contour branch checks agreement around the border in four sectors. Other branches sample the interior at several poses and compare both ink and paper with each ideal symbol. They use both pose-specific normalization and local intensity ranges so that the result depends less on illumination. The final score combines these matches with a separate reading of the speed-limit numerals and, for cancellation signs, the markings on either side of the diagonal stripe. No training photos, external fonts or other assets enter the weight builder, and no coefficients were fitted.
