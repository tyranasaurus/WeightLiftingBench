The builder draws 1,920 individual templates per class from hand-authored 3-D shape models, using a fixed seed to vary view, pose, paint, lighting, and background. Each drawing is independently converted to a descriptor of oriented edges, light–dark structure, and opponent color; descriptors from different drawings are never averaged or fitted together. At inference, `predict` combines the strongest individual template similarities with hand-written evidence for cues such as sky versus water, foliage, wheels, and facial landmarks. No training photo or statistic supplies any constant. I used one labeled check after writing the classifier and did not tune it against the results.
