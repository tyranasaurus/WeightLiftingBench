The detector searches several sizes for localized skin-coloured regions, face relief (eye sockets, nose, and mouth), and head–shoulder–leg arrangements. It compares oriented edges and foreground/background colour differences with individual hand-drawn pose templates; a competing straight-column shape discounts some upright objects. A hand-written decision rule combines these matches with broader cues such as clothing colour below a possible head, interrupted playing surfaces, and long or repeated architectural edges.

The build constructs each template directly from anatomical proportions, ellipses, tapered limbs, or smooth facial bumps. No training pixels, descriptors, fitted parameters, or external weights are included. The 200 labeled photos were inspected visually and used for the five permitted aggregate sandbox checks; the final check was 123/200 correct. This is a development check, not a held-out accuracy estimate.
