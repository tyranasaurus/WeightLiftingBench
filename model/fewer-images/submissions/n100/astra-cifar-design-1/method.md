The network combines object shape with scene context. A fixed-seed drawing program uses polygons, ellipses, and line segments to depict the ten classes at different poses, sizes, and positions. Each of its 15,360 scenes becomes one template: its coarse opponent-color layout, center–surround detail, and pooled edge directions are normalized using only that scene. At inference the network matches those three bands jointly, then combines the best match for each class with the mean of the best matches in six groups. Separate hand-written rules look for cues such as vegetation around a brown body, horizontal lines above water, or paired wheels under a vehicle. All rule weights and filter constants were specified without fitting or calibration on either labeled photos or generated scenes.
