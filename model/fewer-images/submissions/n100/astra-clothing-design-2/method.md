The network compares each input with individually normalized drawings from hand-written sewing patterns, trouser legs, bag bodies and handles, and side-view shoe models. Each drawing contributes its own silhouette and row/column projections; a fixed seed varies garment cut, dimensions, and framing, without combining drawings into prototypes. A second branch uses hand-specified filters and rules to look for details such as persistent front seams, sleeve gaps, patterned fabric, a raised heel, and the open space under sandal straps. Related cues have a bounded combined vote so that, for example, several detectors of the same opening cannot overwhelm the silhouette. The builder reads no images and performs no fitting. The final labeled check classified 666 of the 1,000 provided examples correctly; that is not a held-out test result.
