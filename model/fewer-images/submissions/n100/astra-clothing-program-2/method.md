The builder draws individual silhouettes from hand-written sewing patterns, shoe profiles, straps, and bag handles, varying their dimensions with a fixed seed. Each template stores its own normalized occupancy and contour descriptor and aspect ratio; no training image or pooled statistic of the drawings is stored. At prediction time the classifier extracts a soft foreground mask, compares the item against the templates, and applies hand-written cues for features such as a center fastening, sleeve and hem shape, trouser-leg separation, and open space around sandal straps. The supplied examples were inspected visually and used for five labeled checks; no parameters were fitted. The final check classified 761 of the 1,000 supplied images correctly, not a measurement on the hidden test set.
