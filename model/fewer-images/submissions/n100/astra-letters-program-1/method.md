The classifier matches each photograph against individual renders of an articulated hand. The data-free builder specifies finger flexion, spread, thumb contacts, and palm orientation for each ASL letter, then varies the hand proportions, camera, and lighting with a fixed seed. It keeps a separate normalized descriptor for every render; none are selected, averaged, or fitted. At prediction time it estimates a soft silhouette from border brightness and local texture and combines it with oriented edges and light–dark creases. It searches translations and scale changes, then checks corresponding finger and grip patches with a small positional tolerance. The supplied photographs were used for qualitative viewing and official labeled checks, not as inputs to the build.
