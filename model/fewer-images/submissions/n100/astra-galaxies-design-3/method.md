The network measures the central galaxy with Gaussian apertures, rotated elliptical matched filters, and soft surface-brightness contours traced along radial rays. Axis ratios, disk cross-sections, central concentration, and separately sky-subtracted bulge and disk colors help distinguish smooth shapes and edge-on galaxies. Other branches look for a saddle between two extended nuclei, asymmetric light and tidal contours, a bar that ends inside a disk, and two-armed patterns that wind with log radius in an inclined disk. Dense layers, ReLUs, and max pooling implement the measurements and hand-written comparisons; all weights are generated from analytic functions, geometry, and explicit constants in `build.py`, without loading data or fitting any parameters. The five permitted labeled checks were used for development; the final refinement was shape-checked, not evaluated on labeled images.
