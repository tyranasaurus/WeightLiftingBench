The model represents each letter as a pose of a hand in 3-D: a rounded palm, a wrist, and jointed fingers and thumb. A fixed-seed renderer varies articulation, proportions, viewpoint, framing, and illumination. Each rendered view supplies only its own template coefficients, normalized within that view; no photograph or statistic across rendered views is used to construct a template.

At inference, Gaussian-derivative filters, orientation competition, contrast compression, and spatial pooling extract signed edges, unsigned edges, and regional brightness. Dense dot products match these features against whole-hand and overlapping part templates. The network pools over possible views separately for each letter, then combines the whole-hand and part scores. All coefficients come from the data-free `build.py`; there is no training step.
