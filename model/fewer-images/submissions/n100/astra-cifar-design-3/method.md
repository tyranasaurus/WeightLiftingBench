The network matches luminance, oriented edge energy, and foreground color contrast against individual hand-drawn geometric models of the ten classes. Each model is rendered from ellipses, polygons, and lines at several seeded variations of pose, size, shading, and position; its descriptor is normalized using only that drawing. Separate branches look for aligned wheel rings and local animal faces, and hand-written rules score regional color and texture cues such as foliage, sky, water, and straight horizontal edges. The class scores add these sources of evidence. No training images or labels enter the builder, and no parameters were fitted.
