The network estimates the sky level and measures the central galaxy at multiple orientations and radii. It compares the light to individually normalized analytic ellipse and disk-plus-bulge templates, and independently measures the size, flattening, and angular structure of brightness contours. Antipodal comparisons, tidal-plume probes, and separated peaks with a shared light envelope distinguish disturbances from mergers; angular Fourier filters compare straight bars with arms that wind across successive rings. A hand-written hierarchy combines these measurements and colour contrasts into ten scores. All weights are generated from formulas, index maps, and fixed decision rules in `build.py`, without fitting parameters or storing examples.
