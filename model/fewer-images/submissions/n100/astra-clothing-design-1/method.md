The network compares a soft foreground mask with individually normalized drawings of garment patterns, trouser legs, footwear profiles, and bags. The drawings come from hand-written polygon rules with a fixed random seed; each drawing supplies one correlation filter, and pooling combines their responses at inference. A second shape branch adjusts for aspect ratio, while analytic filters and hand-written rules detect cues such as plackets, cuffs, printed fabric, and open space under sandal straps. Overall silhouette decides the garment family; finer cues rank its members. The builder reads no data and uses no fitted parameters.
