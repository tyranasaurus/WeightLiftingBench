The builder draws each digit from hand-written Bézier pen paths, varying slant, curvature, proportions, pressure, and stroke width with a fixed random seed. Each drawing is centered by its own ink and becomes one template: its signed edge responses, unsigned edge responses, and coarse ink map are pooled and normalized independently of every other drawing. A second view corrects slant and width: fixed moment tests choose a horizontal shear and scale separately for each digit, and sparse interpolation matrices resample the ink. In the corrected view, eight directional scans also measure which pockets of background are surrounded by ink and which open to one side. The network compares both views of the input to each individual template by normalized correlation and combines the best match for each class with the mean of the best matches in eight groups. A separate branch floods background cells from the image border and gives a small class-specific adjustment for enclosed spaces near the top, middle, or bottom of the digit. The builder uses no training images, labels, external fonts, or learned parameters.
