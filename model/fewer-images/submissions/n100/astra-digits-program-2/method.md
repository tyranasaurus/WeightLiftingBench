The build starts from hand-written Bézier pen paths for each digit, including alternative constructions such as crossed sevens and looped twos. With a fixed seed it varies the strokes, proportions, slant, nib width, and pressure. Each drawing becomes one independently normalized template; no training images, class averages, fitted parameters, or external assets are used.

At prediction time the program corrects slant and partially normalizes aspect ratio. It compares local edge directions, ink density, bays, enclosed spaces, and the directions, tips, and junctions of thinned strokes. The closest candidates for each digit get a second comparison that lets small overlapping patches shift with a displacement penalty. The class with the lowest weighted distance to its leading matches wins.
