The network matches the outer sign and its central symbol at a shared candidate position and size. A piecewise-linear tone curve and per-photo luminance and opponent-colour normalization reduce sensitivity to lighting; a parallel branch normalizes just the central crop, so a bright background need not drown out a dark symbol. Its filters come from explicit geometry: rings, triangular rims, arrows, stroked numeral glyphs, vehicle silhouettes, and warning pictograms. Separate part detectors read numerals, warning symbols, and the two ends of a cancellation stripe; max pooling allows small shifts and changes in appearance. Each rendered template is normalized individually. The builder uses no image files, font files, fitted weights, or dataset statistics.
