The network searches for people at several scales using two complementary cues. The face filters look for an oval with plausible skin chromaticity, darker eyes and mouth, and a contrasting surround. The body filters look for oriented contours and colour differences across individually constructed silhouettes in standing, walking, athletic, seated and upper-body poses; separate shirt and trouser tests compare each region against both sides of the background. The readout combines the strongest matches with weaker votes for agreement across scales and discounts scenes with many long, straight edges.

All filters, poses and vote weights are specified in `build.py` by formulas and hand-chosen constants. The build reads no examples or external weights. The one labeled check was made after fixing the weights, with no subsequent tuning.
