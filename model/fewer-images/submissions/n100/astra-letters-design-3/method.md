I specified each letter as a right-hand pose: a palm made of ellipsoids, tapered finger bones with joint angles, a thumb position, and a wrist direction. A fixed-seed renderer varies hand proportions, camera angle, square crop, and light direction. Every render contributes its own independently normalized template; its foreground mask limits how much the template expects from the background.

The network compares two kinds of evidence for each pose: locally thresholded directional edges and finger shafts detected by oriented filters of both polarities. A second branch lets four overlapping parts of a template move by one feature cell before combining their matches. Each letter's score combines its strongest full-hand and elastic matches with votes from fixed pose subgroups. No image, descriptor, class statistic, or fitted weight from the training set is in the builder.
