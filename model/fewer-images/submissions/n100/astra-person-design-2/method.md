The network searches an image pyramid for heads, faces, and upright figures. Soft RGB inequalities locate possible skin, but a head gets more support when it differs from its surround, has the expected hair or eye-and-cheek contrasts, and sits above a distinct garment with shoulders on both sides. A separate detector looks for aligned head, shoulder, hip, and leg contrasts of either polarity, so it can detect a silhouette without visible skin. A fixed readout combines local maxima and regional evidence with weaker scene cues, including sports fields, vivid clothing, long straight edges, and plate rims.

All weights come from hand-specified colour inequalities, Gaussian sampling masks, oriented derivatives, geometric body contours, and decision rules in `build.py`. The builder takes only an output directory; it reads no images, labels, or saved parameters. The supplied labeled examples were used for inspection and the official checks, not for estimating weights.
