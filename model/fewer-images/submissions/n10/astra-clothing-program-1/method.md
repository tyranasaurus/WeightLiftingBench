The data-free builder draws individual garment silhouettes from sleeves, necklines, bodices, skirts and trouser legs; footwear from soles, uppers, heels and straps; and bags from pouches and handles. It uses a fixed seed to vary the dimensions and styles, and draws fabric details such as collars and fasteners on individual upper garments. No template is averaged with another or derived from a supplied photograph.

`predict` isolates the foreground, compares its outline and aspect ratio with the drawings in both orientations, and adds hand-written evidence for features such as a leg fork, handle opening, ankle shaft or shirt placket. A second decision among similar garments uses local gradient orientations, cuffs, hems and front closures. I inspected the supplied examples visually and used the five permitted labeled checks; there was no training or numerical fitting. The final check classified 70 of 100 supplied examples correctly, with shirts the largest source of errors; held-out accuracy is unknown.
