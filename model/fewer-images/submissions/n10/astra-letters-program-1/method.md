The reference hand is an articulated 3D model: ellipsoids form the palm, and tapered round segments form the wrist, fingers and thumb. Each letter specifies which joints bend, which digits touch or spread, and how the palm faces the camera. `build.py` varies hand proportions, viewing angle, light and framing with a fixed seed, and records descriptors and oriented contour points for each individual rendering. No reference is an average of renderings or is computed from a training photo.

At inference, the classifier estimates foreground evidence from the range of intensities along paths from the image border. It searches the reference views using this evidence and coarse gradient directions, then verifies the candidates against oriented contour points, finer gradients and local shading. The highest-scoring letter wins. All comparisons are per image; the other photos in the batch are not used as evidence.
