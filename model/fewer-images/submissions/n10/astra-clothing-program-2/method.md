The builder draws individual garment cutouts from hand-written geometry: sleeves and torso panels, trouser legs, skirts, soles, straps, shafts, and bags with handles. It renders each cutout at sensor scale and records that cutout's own aspect ratio, occupancy grid, and contour projections. No training examples or cross-template statistics are used to construct the constants.

At inference, two foreground masks are compared with the individual templates. The first decision separates upper garments, trousers, dresses, footwear, and bags; within the ambiguous families, hand-written tests look for features such as collars, front fastenings, curved hems, ankle openings, and heel clearance. The reference images were viewed as a labeled gallery and used for the five official checks, not for fitting. The final check classified 70 of the 100 reference images correctly; held-out accuracy is unknown.
