The network searches a pyramid with two-, four-, and eight-pixel cells. One branch looks for an oval patch of plausible skin color, darker eye sockets and mouth, and a lighter nose bridge; another can recognize the paired facial contrasts without skin color. Other branches compare unsigned directional edges with head–shoulder and standing outlines, or look for a second exposed patch where a hand or forearm could be. The decision favors the strongest local match, with smaller votes from repeated matches and agreement between detectors; widespread warmth is discounted so that a room's lighting is not mistaken for a face.

`build.py` constructs every filter from RGB inequalities, Gaussian regions, derivatives, and hand-specified anatomical proportions. All weights and decision thresholds were set without fitting, scoring synthetic scenes, or consulting the labeled examples. The labeled set is used only for a final check.
