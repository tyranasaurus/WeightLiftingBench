The classifier searches each photo for sign outlines using analytic colour and brightness filters, then refines the position, size and tilt by sampling the border. Within each candidate outline, it compares the rectified face and its interior against hand-drawn symbols using brightness, colour, gradient direction and partial-symbol correlations. Extra tests look for features such as the orientation of a triangle, the diagonal stripe on cancellation signs, and the absence of ink on a no-vehicles sign.

`build.py` constructs the drawings from geometric primitives and stroke descriptions, including the numerals; it computes each template's descriptors independently. It does not read images, fonts or other assets, fit parameters, or use random numbers. The final permitted check was 399/430 (92.79%) on the supplied examples; no hidden-test or baseline comparison was available.
