The network compares the photograph against individual views of ten hand-built 3D object models: for example, a car has a glazed cabin and paired wheels, whereas a horse has articulated legs, a narrow head, and a mane. A fixed-seed renderer varies pose, proportions, framing, materials, and background. Each rendered view independently supplies one template of coarse luminance, oriented edge energy, and opponent color. Whole-object templates capture scene layout, while part templates (such as an animal’s head or a truck cab) are matched at 16 locations with local contrast normalization. The network pools **match scores**, not images or template weights. Separate hand-written branches vote from scene cues such as sky versus foliage, paired wheel-like rings, and bilateral face symmetry. The builder uses no data files, training, or fitted parameters.
