I described each letter by the flexion and spread of the four fingers, the thumb's position relative to them, and the orientation of the hand. The builder draws seeded variations of this articulated 3-D model with tapered phalanges, an ellipsoidal palm, a wrist, and directional shading. Each crop becomes one template: its weights encode only its own normalized coarse tones and pooled filter responses, including signed and unsigned edge orientation and center-surround contrast. A finer orientation grid also represents smaller details such as fingertip separations. At inference the network matches these features, maximizes over poses at each scale (and over crop offsets at the coarser scale), and averages the best matches from four independent groups per letter. No supplied photo, label, or statistic of multiple renders is used to produce weights.
