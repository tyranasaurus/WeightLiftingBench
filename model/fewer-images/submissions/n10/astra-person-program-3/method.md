The classifier scans a range of head sizes and aspect ratios, looking for a head above wider shoulders, locally coherent clothing, and facial contrast such as paired dark eyes above lighter cheeks. It corroborates these candidates with oriented gradients along hand-drawn body outlines and with separate skin-colored regions in plausible head-and-hand positions. It pools nearby detections so that the same head does not count repeatedly, then makes a small adjustment for scene cues such as grass, snow, or strong architectural lines.

The geometry, color formulas, weights, and decision rules were specified by hand from general knowledge, without fitting to or extracting constants from examples. `build.py` writes the entire NumPy implementation from embedded source and needs neither input data nor a constants file.
