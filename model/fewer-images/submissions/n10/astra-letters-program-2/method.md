The builder poses an articulated right hand for each letter using hand-written joint angles and thumb paths. For side-facing signs, it also renders poses specified by the direction and foreshortening of each finger segment. It renders overlapping ellipsoids with a depth buffer, directional lighting, and contact shadows, varying the anatomy, camera, crop, and light with a fixed seed. Each render supplies one independently normalized template; no feature is averaged across renders, and no photo is used by the builder.

At inference, the classifier compares local edge orientations, contrast, shading, and estimated foreground occupancy at several translations. It then checks the strongest candidates against directed edge probes and signed brightness comparisons around the projected thumb, fingertips, and knuckles. The directed probes ask whether the expected edges are present, so an unrelated background edge need not count as a mismatch.
