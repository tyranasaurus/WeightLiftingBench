The network estimates the sky from a clipped outer annulus, recenters the galaxy using aperture-weighted light centroids, and measures the shape and color of its inner and outer light. It compares the image with individually normalized analytic profiles of spheroids, discs, and disc-plus-bar or disc-plus-bulge systems, allowing orientation, inclination, and size to vary. Angular Fourier filters on concentric ellipses measure arm winding and contour asymmetry; a separate branch looks for distinct nuclei with resolved surrounding light and a luminous bridge. Handwritten piecewise-linear rules combine these measurements into the ten class scores. All weights are generated from geometry, profile formulas, and hand-chosen constants in `build.py`, without a fitted readout or stored image examples.
