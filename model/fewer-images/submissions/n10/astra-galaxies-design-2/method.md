The network subtracts a per-image estimate of the sky and compares the central light distribution with individually normalized, analytic templates for spheroids, disks with and without bulges, bars, spirals, tidal tails, and paired nuclei. Max pooling searches over orientation, apparent size, and shape; competing single- and two-component models use the same aperture. Other branches measure point symmetry, radial color gradients, angular winding, off-plane light, and the prominence of extended secondary peaks. A hand-written piecewise-linear decision rule combines the measurements into ten scores. All weights are generated by `build.py` from formulas and fixed morphology assumptions, without loading images or fitting parameters.
