The classifier measures the central galaxy independently in each image. It subtracts the local sky, estimates the seeing from field sources, and measures connected isophotes (contours of equal brightness) to obtain axis ratios, orientations and a size scale. It compares the light with nested ellipses, measures one-sided tidal extensions, and looks for resolved secondary nuclei with light between the two cores. In the disk plane, signed logarithmic-spiral filters measure curved arms, while a persistent inner elongation in red light indicates a bar. For edge-on systems it compares the central brightness and thickness against the disk away from the centre.

The filters, geometric tests, and decision rules were written by hand, without a fit, template bank, or precomputed weights. I inspected the example galleries and used the allowed labeled sandbox checks to diagnose qualitative failure modes. `build.py` emits the classifier from its embedded source without reading any files; no `constants.npz` is needed.
