The network scans an image pyramid for two kinds of local evidence: a face, and a head above a clothed torso. The face filters look for a localized patch of plausible skin color, an oval boundary, and the arrangement of darker eyes and mouth around lighter cheeks and nose. Separate rules can use facial contrast without skin color, or detect a clothed figure using shoulder, torso and leg contours plus the contrast between a shirt and both of its flanks. The readout favors the strongest detection but also gives weight to repeated detections and uses scene color and long straight lines as weaker context cues.

All filters, geometric proportions, thresholds and voting weights are specified in `build.py` using color inequalities, Gaussian sampling regions and sketches of anatomical contours in dimensionless coordinates. No photographs, learned weights, statistics over generated examples or optimization are used to construct the network.
