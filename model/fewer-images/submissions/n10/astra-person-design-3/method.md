The network scans an image pyramid for a head and for a head above a wider torso. It combines two soft skin-colour tests under bounded colour-cast correction with hand-drawn-in-formula facial contrasts: eyes below the forehead, brighter cheeks beside the nose, and a darker mouth above the chin. Separate filters look for shoulder slopes, limbs, and a compact silhouette in either brightness polarity, so colour is not required. The final score combines the strongest local detection with agreement between cues, repeated detections, and a weak prior on where skin-coloured regions occur. All weights and thresholds come from geometry, analytic filters, and hand-written rules; no examples or fitted parameters enter the build.
