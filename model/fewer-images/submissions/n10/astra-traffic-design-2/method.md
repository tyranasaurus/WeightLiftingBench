The network first compresses RGB intensities and extracts normalized luminance, red/blue opponent colors, and luminance detail. Its filters come from analytic rims, triangles and diamonds, and from individual supersampled vector drawings of numerals, vehicles, arrows and warning symbols; each drawing is normalized using only its own support. Convolutions and max pooling search several positions, scales and aspect ratios. Some readers score a border and its inscription at the same pose, while others read the distinguishing digit or look beside a cancellation stripe. The final scores combine whole-sign evidence with fine-detail comparisons within each family. The builder uses no data, external fonts, fitted coefficients or stored example images.
