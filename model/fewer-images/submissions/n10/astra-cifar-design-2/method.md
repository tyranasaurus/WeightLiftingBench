The shape branch compares pooled luminance, directional derivatives, edge orientations, and spot responses against 768 independent drawings per class. Each drawing comes from hand-specified geometry (for example, an airliner's swept wings, a ship's hull, or a frog's folded hind legs); its descriptor is centered and normalized using that drawing alone. The network pools match scores across variations in pose, scale, position, and lighting. Other branches use hand-written rules for regional colors and textures, paired wheels, and eye–nose arrangements. The builder uses only formulas and a fixed random seed; no photographs, labels, training, or calibration enter the weights.
