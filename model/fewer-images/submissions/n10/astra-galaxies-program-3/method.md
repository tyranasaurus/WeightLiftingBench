The classifier locates the central nucleus, subtracts a robust sky level, and measures the shapes of equal-brightness contours. It samples deprojected rings to distinguish a bar with a fixed position angle from spiral arms whose angle changes with radius. Separate tests look for attached asymmetric tails, a second resolved nucleus with a shared halo, and the narrow disk around a thicker central bulge. The edge-on test also compares the contours with individually normalized analytic spheroid and disk-plus-bulge templates.

All filters, templates, and decision rules were written by hand. The labeled examples were used for visual inspection and the five permitted diagnostic checks, not to generate templates or fit coefficients. `build.py` writes the standalone NumPy solution without reading any data; no constants file is needed. Each image is processed independently, with no randomness or batch normalization.
