The detector searches face-sized windows for skin-like chroma isolated from the surroundings, a bilateral head outline, darker eyes above lighter cheeks, and shoulders below the head. It checks promising windows against individually normalized analytic portrait stencils and also searches for a head, wider garment, and narrowing legs with coherent color and contrast on both sides. Distinct head candidates can corroborate one another; scene-wide color and straight-edge cues have much smaller votes. All geometry, color rules, and decision weights were written by hand, and `build.py` draws each portrait stencil directly from formulas without reading any data or fitting parameters.
