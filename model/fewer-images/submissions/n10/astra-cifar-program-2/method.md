`build.py` constructs the ten object classes from hand-authored geometry: for example, cars have wheels and a glazed cabin, birds have wings and a bill, and frogs have folded hind legs and raised eyes. A fixed-seed renderer varies viewpoint, framing, materials, lighting, and background. Each rendered image is a separate template encoded using its own normalized gradient, shading, and colour descriptors; no templates are averaged and no parameters are fitted.

`predict` compares an input and its reflection to those templates, both as a whole and in three fixed subregions. It combines the matches with hand-written scene cues such as straight edges, foliage, road surfaces, and horizontal water texture. The provided photos were reserved for the final labeled check, not used to build or select the classifier.
