The network compares soft foreground occupancy and grayscale tone with 1,536 individual procedural drawings per class. Each drawing uses hand-specified parts (such as a bodice and sleeves, two trouser legs, a toe and heel joined by a sole, or a bag and handle), with proportions and pose varied by a fixed random seed. Each template is normalized using that drawing alone; the network pools matches over alternative views to score each silhouette. Analytic filters then distinguish garments by fastenings, collars, hems, and fabric contrast, and footwear by lacing and open spaces above the sole. These detail votes rank classes within a family without changing the family's strongest silhouette score. The builder reads no data and performs no fitting.
