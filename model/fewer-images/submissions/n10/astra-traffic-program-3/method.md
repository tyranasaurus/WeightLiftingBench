The classifier searches for a circle, triangle, octagon, or diamond using luminance and opponent colour channels. It refines each candidate’s position, scale, and shear against its border, rectifies the sign, and compares both the whole sign and its interior with hand-drawn templates. The interior comparison combines contrast-normalized intensity with pooled signed gradients; speed-limit signs also get a digit-by-digit reading constrained to a common size and baseline.

The build draws the motifs from geometric primitives and pen paths. Each template and its descriptors are computed independently; no external artwork, fitted weights, or image samples are included. I inspected the supplied examples visually and used the five permitted labeled checks. The final check classified 385/430 examples correctly (89.53%); these are not held-out results.
