I described each digit as a small grammar of Bézier pen paths, including variants such as an open or closed 4 and a 7 with or without a crossbar. A fixed random seed varies the aspect ratio, slant, curvature, and pen pressure; each drawing is scaled to the stated bounding box and centered on its own ink centroid. Gaussian derivative, ridge, and smoothing filters measure stroke direction and ink placement in overlapping cells. Each drawing supplies one individually normalized template in that space; the network combines the best correlation with the mean of eight group-best correlations. A separate branch floods the background from the frame edges and uses the area and vertical position of unreached spaces as evidence for loops. No training examples, fonts, fitted parameters, or statistics across drawings are used to construct the weights.
