The network matches a sign's outline and printed symbol at the same candidate position, width and height, then takes the best joint match for each class. A piecewise-linear approximation to logarithmic RGB, opponent-colour channels and a local estimate of paper brightness help preserve contrast in dark or unevenly lit crops. Outline filters describe rings, discs, triangles, a diamond and an octagon; interior filters come from individually rasterized geometric drawings of numerals, vehicles, arrows and warning pictograms. Additional branches match individual digits, the upper and lower portions of warning symbols, and the visible ink on either side of a cancellation stripe. All weights come from these hand-specified drawings, formulas and decision rules in `build.py`; no image data, external fonts, learned weights or fitting procedure are used by the builder.
