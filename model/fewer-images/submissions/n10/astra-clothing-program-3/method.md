The builder draws independent silhouettes from hand-written garment cuts, shoe profiles, and bag bodies and handles. It rasterizes each drawing at the resolution implied by its own aspect ratio; it reads no examples, and no templates are averaged or fitted. The classifier compares a photo's soft foreground mask, horizontal and vertical envelopes, and aspect ratio against individual drawings in both orientations. It first distinguishes trousers, upper-body garments and dresses, footwear, and bags, then uses hand-written cues such as leg separation, sleeve ends, center openings, collar tips, open straps, and boot cuffs to distinguish similar outlines. All randomness is confined to template construction and uses a fixed seed.
