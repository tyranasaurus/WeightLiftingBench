The classifier matches local edge, ridge, and fingertip evidence against individual rendered right-hand poses. I specified the palm, wrist, thumb opposition, finger joint flexion and finger spread by hand for the 24 static letters; a fixed random seed varies anatomy, camera angle, crop, lighting and sleeve. Gaussian derivative convolutions and a center-surround filter supply the image features, and a spatial normalization layer normalizes each local histogram against its own contrast. Each render supplies one template, split between a fine finger-shape descriptor and a coarser edge descriptor. Both are normalized from that render alone, and the network adds their cosine-match scores before comparing poses. For each letter, the network takes the strongest match in each of four groups of views and averages those four responses. No examples or image files are read by the builder, and no weights were fitted.
