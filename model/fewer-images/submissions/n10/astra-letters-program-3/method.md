I specified the static letters as joint angles, thumb contacts, and hand orientations in a palm-local coordinate system. `build.py` ray-casts these articulated hands with a fixed seed, varying anatomy, camera framing, and lighting; each render is stored as its own independently normalized template. At inference, the program estimates foreground from texture and the consistency of smooth background patches, then compares unsigned image gradients, foreground occupancy, and signed contour directions across small translations and scales. A coarse comparison selects candidate poses for a finer comparison that emphasizes each candidate's hand area and checks skin shading and enclosed gaps between digits. I used the provided photos to understand the sign presentation and the official labeled runner to check the implementation; no photo measurements, class statistics, or fitted parameters are included in the templates.
