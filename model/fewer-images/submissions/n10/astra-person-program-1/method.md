The classifier scans for heads at several sizes and aspect ratios. It proposes candidate locations using colour, local contrast and shoulder geometry, then tests them for a hairline, an enclosed head contour, face-like shading, a widening torso, and possible exposed arms or hands. A bank of analytic face patterns represents the eye sockets, cheeks, nose and mouth; each pattern is normalized independently, and matching removes a linear illumination gradient. Separate scoring rules allow for missing skin colour or missing facial detail. The geometry, colour bands and decision rules were written by hand from general anatomy and image-processing principles; the build uses no data files, learned weights or randomness.
