The network adds three scores for each class: whole-object shape, local parts, and scene context. For shape, `build.py` draws 480 poses per class from hand-authored polygons and ellipses (for example, a car body with two wheels or a bird with a beak and tail). Each drawing becomes one template, normalized independently using only its own luminance, band-pass contrast, and oriented edges; no measurements are pooled across drawings to set weights. At inference, max pooling allows several poses to explain an object, while a separate branch looks for local arrangements such as two tires over a road or eyes above a muzzle. The context branch combines soft color and texture rules, such as blue below a horizontal hull or green outside a brown animal. All parameters come from fixed formulas, hand-specified rules, and a fixed pose-generation seed; no weights were fitted.
