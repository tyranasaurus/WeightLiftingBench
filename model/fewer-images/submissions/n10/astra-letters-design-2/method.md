I specified each ASL handshape in relative palm units, using a palm and forearm, tapered finger segments, joint flexion, and thumb opposition. A fixed-seed renderer varies proportions, viewing angle, framing, and illumination; it includes a second construction of curled fingers in the palm’s picture plane. Each render supplies an individual template of pooled signed gradients, polarity-independent fine and coarse orientations, ridge responses, low-frequency luminance, and center-surround contrast. These features are centered and normalized within that template alone. The network applies the same fixed filters to the photograph, scores agreement with individual templates, and combines within-letter maxima from four pose groups. No photograph or label supplies a weight, and no template is averaged or selected by testing it against other images.
