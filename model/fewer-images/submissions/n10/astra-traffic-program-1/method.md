The classifier locates candidate sign outlines by comparing log-color and brightness on opposite sides of a hand-defined border, then refines the center, scale, and shear. In the resulting sign coordinates it compares the interior against hand-drawn digits, arrows, and pictograms using normalized intensity and pooled signed gradients. Speed limits also have an ordered glyph comparison; colored lamps, paired vehicles, blank interiors, and the white no-entry bar have additional checks. `build.py` constructs each template directly from geometric primitives and normalizes it independently. It uses no photo files, learned parameters, or external artwork.
