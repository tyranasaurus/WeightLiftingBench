The network combines normalized luminance with a soft foreground mask to match clothing silhouettes at 14 × 14 resolution. Its dense layer contains 2,048 individually normalized drawings per class: for example, a trouser has two tapering legs, a bag has a body and optional arched handle, and a sandal has a footbed with separate straps. A fixed seed varies the dimensions, pose, and details of each drawing within hand-specified ranges; no drawings are averaged to create weights. At inference, the network pools template matches and adds hand-written scores for cues such as vertical fastenings, lapels, detached sleeves, fabric texture, and open straps. Silhouette scores choose the broad group; detail cues reorder classes within the upper-garment and footwear groups without overriding that group decision. The build reads no examples or labels and does not fit any parameters.
