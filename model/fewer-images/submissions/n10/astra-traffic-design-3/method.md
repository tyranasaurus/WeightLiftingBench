The network searches for each sign at ten fixed sizes/aspect ratios and 25 center positions. It matches the colored frame separately from the interior drawing, using exposure-normalized luminance, red/blue/yellow opponent channels, and local edge contrast. A fine-stroke reader distinguishes speed-limit numerals; its output is centered across the candidate limits at inference so that a generic dark mark does not count as evidence for a speed limit. Separate filters look for both ends of a cancellation stripe and read the emblem outside the stripe.

All shape, numeral, and pictogram filters come from vector paths and geometric formulas in `build.py`. Each drawing or window is normalized independently. The remaining weights implement fixed interpolation, filters, pooling, and hand-specified decision rules; none were fitted or copied from photographs.
