The classifier compares each photo with individual renderings of hand-built 3D shapes: for example, a car has two axles and sloped windows, while a frog has raised eyes and folded hind legs. The builder varies viewpoint, scale, materials, and background with a fixed seed. Each rendering is encoded independently using local edge orientations, signed luminance gradients, coarse shading, and colour differences. At prediction time, the strongest matches for each class are combined with hand-written scene cues such as blue water below a hull, vegetation around an animal, or straight edges above a road. No training photos, fitted weights, or statistics across renderings enter the build.
