The main branch measures eight signed edge directions and local ink density on a 7×7 grid. Each template is one rendering of a hand-written pen program: the fixed-seed generator varies slant, proportions, curvature, and pen width, then centers that drawing by its own ink mass. A dense row stores only that drawing’s centered, unit-length feature vector at a fixed scale. The classifier averages the best cosine matches from four groups per digit. A separate min/max flood fill measures enclosed white space above and below the image center and adds a small, hand-specified correction to the class scores. No data were used to generate, select, or calibrate the weights.
