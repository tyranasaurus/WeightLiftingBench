The shape branch compares coarse luminance, oriented edges, and colour contrast with 640 independently drawn templates per class. Each drawing comes from a hand-written geometric model—for example, a car has a glazed cabin above two wheels, while a deer has a narrow neck and four long legs—and each template uses only its own drawing for normalization. The network also checks local arrangements such as eyes above a muzzle, and combines these with hand-written rules about where it sees sky, water, vegetation, smooth surfaces, or straight edges. All weights come from those rules, analytic filters, and a fixed random seed for pose and appearance; none were fit or selected using photos or synthetic evaluation runs.
