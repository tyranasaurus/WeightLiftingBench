The detector searches for a head at eight heights and three aspect ratios to account for the photos being squashed. At each position it checks colour and brightness changes around the eyes, cheeks and hairline, and measures whether image edges follow a rounded head, narrowing neck and widening shoulders. A separate rule looks for a coherent shirt above two similarly coloured legs; neither that rule nor the silhouette rule requires skin colour. A second plausible detection elsewhere in the photo and a few weak scene cues can support the strongest local detection.

I wrote the geometry, colour formulas and decision thresholds by hand from general knowledge. The builder writes the complete detector from its own source, without reading images, loading weights or fitting parameters.
