The classifier estimates the local sky level, locates the central galaxy, and traces nested brightness contours along rays. Their shapes give orientation, axis ratio, pointedness, and asymmetry; folded minor-axis profiles provide evidence for an edge-on bulge, while a resolved secondary peak and a luminous bridge provide evidence for a merger. In deprojected polar coordinates, it uses angular Fourier modes to distinguish a persistent central bar from winding arms, estimates arm pitch with logarithmic-spiral filters, and combines these measurements with radial color changes in hand-written decision rules.

All filters and decisions were written from image-processing and galaxy-morphology considerations. No weights or thresholds were fitted, no training-image measurements were copied, and no constants file is needed. The ten labeled images were viewed for context and used for one sandbox check, not for tuning.
