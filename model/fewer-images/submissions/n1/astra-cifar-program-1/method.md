The builder draws separate grayscale templates for ten object families from polygons, ellipses and line segments. Deterministic variations cover viewpoints, lighting, reflection, size and position. Each drawing supplies its own normalized descriptors of oriented edges, local brightness and silhouette; no statistics are estimated across drawings. At prediction time the program matches these descriptors against each photograph, estimating its silhouette from color differences with its borders. Handwritten rules add evidence from surrounding sky, vegetation, water or pavement, as well as features such as paired wheels, a face-like eye arrangement and long straight edges. No photograph or label is used to build the classifier or set its parameters.
