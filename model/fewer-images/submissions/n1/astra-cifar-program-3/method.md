The classifier compares each photo with multiple projected views of hand-built 3D models. The models encode parts such as tires and windows, hulls and sails, wings, muzzles, ears, and jointed legs. Each rendering becomes its own normalized template of oriented edges, luminance, and silhouette; no template is averaged with another. At inference time the program searches small changes in crop, scale, and reflection and combines pose matches with hand-written cues for sky, water, vegetation, roads, face symmetry, and paired wheels. All geometry, colors, filters, and decision rules are specified in `build.py`, without fitting or reading any example photos.
