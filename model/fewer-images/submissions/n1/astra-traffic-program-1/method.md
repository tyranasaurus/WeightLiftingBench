The classifier first searches for rounded triangular borders, rings, discs, and diamonds using log-colour ratios and local brightness contrast. It rectifies each candidate and matches the interior against independently normalized drawings of digits, arrows, vehicles, pedestrians, and other sign symbols. It combines whole-symbol correlation with fine-detail matching, a distance-tolerant stroke score for warning signs, and a separate glyph reader for speed limits. A blank regulation sign must also pass a test for the absence of printed ink.

`build.py` draws the stencils from hand-written geometric rules and produces the NumPy-only predictor and its constants without reading any data files. No model was trained or fitted. The supplied photos were used for visual inspection and three labeled functionality checks, not as templates or sources of numeric measurements.
