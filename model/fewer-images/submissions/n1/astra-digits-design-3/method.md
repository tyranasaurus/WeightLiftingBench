The network compares each digit against 6,144 independently drawn templates per class. The builder starts from hand-written Bézier pen paths, varies their proportions, slant, curvature and pen pressure with a fixed seed, and centers each drawing on its own ink centroid. Each template contributes three individually normalized descriptors: pooled ink, signed edge responses, and stroke-ridge responses. The class score combines the closest match with support from other groups of templates and two small geometric cues: an exterior flood fill locates enclosed background, and local Euler-characteristic filters detect evidence of a second loop. The filters, score weights and shape recipes are specified in code; no images or statistics from the supplied examples are used to construct them.
