`build.py` describes the 24 signs by which fingers extend, how the joints bend, where the thumb meets the fingers, and how the palm faces the viewer. A fixed-seed renderer projects rounded palm, finger, and forearm parts under varied viewpoint, framing, and lighting. Each rendering supplies one independent template; no rendering is averaged with another, and no parameters or templates are fitted to the supplied photos.

`predict` compares contrast-insensitive edge orientations and foreground–background contrast at two resolutions. It first searches several whole-image alignments, then compares small patches around each shortlisted pose, allowing a patch to move by one fine-grid cell. The returned integer is the class whose strongest individual matches agree best with the input. Prediction uses no randomness or information from other images in the batch.
