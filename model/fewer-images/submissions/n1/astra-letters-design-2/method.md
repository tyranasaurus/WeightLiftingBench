The hand model uses four articulated finger chains, an opposable thumb, an ellipsoidal palm and a wrist. For each sign I specified finger flexion, spread, contact points and wrist orientation from the ASL handshape, then rendered independent variations in anatomy, pose, crop and illumination with a fixed seed. Each render supplies exactly one template, normalized on its own; no pose is selected by scoring it, and no statistics are taken across renders. The network compares oriented contrast and coarse grayscale features with each template. It pools those similarities within each sign, combining the best match with the mean of eight subgroup maxima to reduce sensitivity to a single accidental match. The builder uses no training data or learned parameters.
