I wrote pen paths for each digit, including variants such as an open or triangular 4 and a crossed or uncrossed 7. A fixed parameter sequence varies the width, slant, curvature, nib pressure and placement; each resulting drawing contributes one template, centered and normalized using only that drawing. The network compares the input with each template by combining two correlations: one on pooled ink density and one on spatially pooled responses to oriented edge and ridge filters. Each class combines its best match with the mean of its group winners. A separate branch counts occupied faces, edges and vertices to estimate the number and vertical location of holes, applying a soft penalty rather than rejecting a digit with a broken loop. All paths, filters, mixing weights and topology rules are specified in `build.py`; none were fitted or selected by scoring images.
