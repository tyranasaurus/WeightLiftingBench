The shape branch compares pooled oriented gradients and coarse luminance against 600 illustrated hypotheses for each class. I specified silhouettes, parts, and simple three-dimensional solids by hand, then varied the view, pose, lighting contrast, and scale with a fixed seed. Each illustration supplies only its own spatially centered, individually normalized template; pooling over matches happens at inference, not during weight construction.

A second branch uses hand-set RGB predicates, regional color and texture, horizontal and vertical edges, mirror symmetry, paired-wheel contrasts, and a two-eyes-and-nose contrast. Handwritten class rules combine these cues with the shape scores. No example photographs, labels, fitted statistics, or optimization are used by the builder.
