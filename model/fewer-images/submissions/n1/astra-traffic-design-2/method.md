The network scores the outer plate and its printed symbol separately: a coarse branch looks for coloured rims, discs, triangles and diamonds, while a finer branch looks for individual numerals, arrows, vehicles and warning pictograms. A piecewise-linear log transform compresses exposure differences; colour-opponent channels, local contrast normalization and centre-surround filtering reduce sensitivity to shadows and background scenery. The warning-sign branch also samples the triangular white field directly, so that the symbol can be compared without the red rim dominating its contrast. Max pooling over hand-specified poses accommodates changes in sign size, translation and slant.

`build.py` constructs every weight from explicit geometry, including stroke paths for the numerals, or from fixed analytic filters and hand-specified scoring rules. Each rendered pattern supplies its own template; no statistics across renderings or supplied photographs are used to produce weights. There is no training step or external asset dependency.
