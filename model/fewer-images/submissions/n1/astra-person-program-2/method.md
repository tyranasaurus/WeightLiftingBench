The detector scans for a dark eye pair separated by a lighter nose bridge, with cheek, forehead, mouth and face-surround checks. It also compares local shading to individual analytic face drawings at different angles. A second scan looks for a compact head above widening shoulders, a coherent garment and possible hands; a third traces oriented edges along hand-specified standing, walking and running outlines. The tests use several sizes and aspect ratios to allow for the resize to a square. The decision combines the strongest matches, evidence for multiple people, and small scene-context adjustments. All geometry, color rules and decision weights were specified by hand without training or copying any images. `build.py` reproduces the detector directly from source and needs no input files.
