The classifier compares pooled, oriented edge and ridge responses, rather than raw brightness. Its shared front end normalizes exposure, applies analytic derivative filters, and compresses strong responses with a fixed piecewise-linear function. The builder poses a right hand made of ellipsoids and tapered segments using ASL articulation rules, and varies the camera, hand proportions, lighting, background brightness, and wrist direction. Each rendering supplies one independently centered and normalized template. Opposite gradient directions partially share evidence, so a contour can match across a contrast reversal. For each letter the classifier adds the strongest correlation to the mean of eight group maxima: a match supported by several poses counts more than an isolated match. No template or parameter is fitted to photographs, and no normalization statistics are shared between renderings.
