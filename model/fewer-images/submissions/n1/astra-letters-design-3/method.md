I set the finger flexion, spread, thumb opposition, and wrist orientation for each ASL letter in a procedural 3-D hand. The renderer varies proportions, pose, crop and illumination from a fixed seed; each rendering supplies just one template, normalized on its own. The network measures oriented edges, center–surround contrasts, and coarse shading, pools them spatially, and correlates the resulting contrast maps with each individual template at nine nearby positions. For each letter it averages the best match from each of four pose groups. No photographs, fitted parameters, or statistics across renderings enter the weights.
