The shape branch converts brightness to soft foreground occupancy and compares the normalized 14 × 14 footprint with 30,720 individual analytic drawings. The drawings vary garment cuts, poses, and proportions: for example, two tapered legs join at a crotch, and a sandal has straps over a footbed rather than a solid upper. Each template is centered and normalized using only its own drawing; class evidence combines the strongest match with the strongest match from each of four fixed groups. A second branch uses fixed contrast filters and hand-written rules for details that silhouettes can miss, such as a central fastening, fabric texture, and open space between straps. The builder uses a fixed random seed and no input data, and no weights or settings were fitted or selected by scoring models.
