I wrote pen paths for common ways of writing each digit, including alternatives such as an open or closed 4 and a crossed or uncrossed 7. A fixed-seed renderer varies the path geometry, pen width, and pressure to make 8,192 individual templates per class. Each template is deskewed and normalized independently, then described by its ink distribution, oriented gradients at two scales, aspect ratio, and enclosed background pockets. At prediction time the program shortlists templates by those descriptors, then compares their ink more closely, allowing small local displacements. The build reads no data files; no template, weight, or threshold was fitted to the provided images or calibrated against generated examples.
