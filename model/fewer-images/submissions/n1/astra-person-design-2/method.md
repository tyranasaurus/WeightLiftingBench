The detector searches an image pyramid for face-sized ellipses of skin-like color, comparing each ellipse with its surround. Separate luminance filters look for darker eyes against cheeks and forehead, a brighter nose bridge, and contrast at the hairline; these can vote even in a grayscale photo. A second branch looks for the outline of a head above sloping shoulders and for a torso whose color is consistent vertically but differs from both sides. The network combines head and torso evidence at corresponding locations and scales, then pools the strongest local votes with small corrections for composition and scene context.

All weights come from RGB inequalities, Gaussian lobes, oriented derivatives, line-segment geometry, and hand-set decision rules in `build.py`. No weights or thresholds were fitted, copied from photos, or selected by scoring generated images.
