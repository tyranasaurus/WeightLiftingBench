The classifier subtracts the local sky background, locates the central light peak, and measures the connected galaxy at several brightness levels. It uses axis ratios for smooth galaxies, a second resolved nucleus and rotational asymmetry for interactions, and changes in vertical thickness and concentration for edge-on bulges. For spirals, it samples deprojected elliptical rings: a fixed two-fold pattern suggests a bar, while a pattern whose angle moves with log radius indicates winding arms and gives an estimate of pitch. All filters and decision rules were written by hand from morphology considerations; the builder reads no data, and the single labeled check was not used to tune the classifier.
