The network searches an image pyramid for faces, head-and-shoulder outlines, and clothed figures. One branch uses soft inequalities on RGB differences to find skin-like colors, then checks whether a candidate contrasts with its immediate surroundings and has darker eyes, hair, or lips in plausible positions. Other branches match oriented luminance edges to hand-drawn anatomical curves and check whether both sides of a torso contrast with the background; these cues can also respond in grayscale photos. The readout combines the best regional matches across scales, with extra weight for a face directly above matching shoulders rather than two matches in unrelated places.

All weights come from geometric and color formulas in `build.py`, with thresholds and voting weights chosen by hand. No photos, pretrained weights, fitting, or calibration on generated images were used to construct the network.
