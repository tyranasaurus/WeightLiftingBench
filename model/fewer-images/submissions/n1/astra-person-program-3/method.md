The classifier scans for heads at eight scales and two aspect ratios. It scores each candidate using skin-colour contrast, the relative brightness of the eyes, nose and cheeks, and the presence of a wider region of clothing below the head. A separate detector matches oriented luminance and colour edges to hand-drawn standing, striding, seated and upper-body outlines; this gives back views and monochrome photos a route to a positive prediction without requiring skin colour. The decision uses the strongest local evidence, with a small bonus for a second spatially distinct candidate. All geometry, colour rules and decision weights were written by hand from general knowledge, without fitting to real or generated images; `build.py` needs no data or separate constants file.
