The network matches two views of the input: a soft foreground mask for shape and a grey-level view for details inside the item. Its dense filters are separate, individually normalized drawings from fixed-seed geometric patterns: bodices and sleeves, bifurcated trouser legs, soles and uppers, and bags with handles. Within each class the network combines the best match and the mean of four group maxima, then adds hand-written evidence for a continuous front opening, fabric texture, left–right asymmetry, and enclosed gaps. The builder reads no images; no parameters were fitted, and no design choices were selected by scoring generated drawings. The labeled check was reserved for the finished network.
