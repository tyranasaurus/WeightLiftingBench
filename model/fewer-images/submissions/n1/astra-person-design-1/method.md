The network scans a pyramid of overlapping windows for three complementary cues: a skin-colored oval above a differently colored garment, the relative brightness of facial regions, and an upright outline with a head, shoulders, torso and legs. It also looks for smaller skin-colored regions where hands or legs could support a head detection. The face-contrast and contour detectors work on luminance and do not require skin color. Each detector combines bounded votes from several parts so that one strong edge cannot stand in for a whole person; the readout favors the best location and gives smaller votes to corroborating locations and scales. Composition, long straight edges and grass-like field coverage provide weak scene cues.

All kernels, color inequalities, geometry and readout coefficients are defined by hand in `build.py`. No weights come from labeled photographs, pretrained models, optimization, or measurements across generated examples.
