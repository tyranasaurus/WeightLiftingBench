The network combines three kinds of evidence: object shape, surrounding scene, and paired wheels. For shape, `build.py` draws 1,152 poses per class from hand-written geometric models; each drawing supplies one template, normalized independently, for oriented edges, coarse luminance, local detail, and silhouette. At inference, the network pools the strongest matching poses and adds hand-written rules for cues such as blue above or below the object, foliage around it, long straight boundaries, and two dark tire rings at a plausible separation. The builder uses a fixed random seed and reads no examples; no weights or rule thresholds were fitted.
