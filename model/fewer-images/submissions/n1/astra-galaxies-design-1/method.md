The network measures the central galaxy in polar coordinates. It estimates the sky from peripheral sectors, samples smoothed light along rays, and tracks the light that remains connected to the center to construct nested isophotes. The angular harmonics of these contours measure flattening, lopsidedness, and changes in orientation; handed log-spiral filters, outward brightness rises, and aperture colors supply complementary evidence for arms, companions, and bulges. The output scores combine these measurements with hand-written piecewise-linear rules. All filters, thresholds, and weights are specified by formulas in `build.py`; none were fitted to images.
