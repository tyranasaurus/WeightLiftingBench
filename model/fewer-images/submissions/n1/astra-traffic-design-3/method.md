The network first compresses brightness with a piecewise-linear logarithm and extracts luminance, local luminance contrast, and opponent colours. It matches the whole sign and its central legend separately: the whole-face templates describe borders and colours, while the higher-resolution legend templates describe digits, vehicles, arrows and warning pictograms. A third branch checks oriented contours and requires them to agree with the legend on pose. Every template is rasterized from geometric primitives in `build.py` and normalized using only its own pixels. Max pooling allows for changes in scale, position and viewing angle; fixed colour rules and a test for a blank interior supplement the matches. No photographic data or fitted parameters are used to build the network.
